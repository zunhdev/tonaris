# Tonaris — G1 spike: Tidal links, 20 October 2026

Spike for [TON-100](https://linear.app/zunhdev/issue/TON-100). On 20 October 2026 the creator made **Tidal a fourth launch service**, beside Spotify, Apple Music and Qobuz. This spike answers what identifying a pasted Tidal link can rely on, and what Tidal's terms allow:

- the link forms (TS-40);
- step 2, MusicBrainz's URL lookup: how often MusicBrainz knows the Tidal link of an album it has (TS-41, as TON-14 measured the others);
- Tidal's terms for its API and its pages;
- step 4, hints from Tidal's album page, measured as [TON-99](2026-10-19-g1-qobuz-hints.md) measured Qobuz's.

## Verdict

**Tidal links are identified by MusicBrainz's URL lookup and by hints from Tidal's album page, never through Tidal's API.**

- **Step 2 is precise but covers half.** MusicBrainz knew the Tidal link of **23 of 50** albums it has (46%), every one the right album. It knew 1 of 25 albums Wikidata doesn't link to MusicBrainz.
- **Tidal's album pages give good hints.** TON-16's rule, restricted to official albums and EPs as G2 proposed, identified **41 of 51** labelled links (80%), and **41 of the 46** with hints (89%). **None was wrong.** It also matched 4 albums Wikidata doesn't link, all of them right when checked by hand.
- **Together, steps 2 and 4 identify 44 of the 50 albums MusicBrainz has (88%)**, against 23 with step 2 alone. Over the whole sample that is 49 of 75 against 24, with no wrong album.
- **Tidal's API is out** without Tidal's written approval. Its terms forbid three things identification would need:
  - sending data to another service (the barcode to MusicBrainz);
  - combining Tidal content with other services;
  - any use "in connection with" AI, which Tonaris's MCP access is.
- **Track links name no album.** Without the API, nothing finds a Tidal track's album. The creator decided that they go to manual entry with the link kept, as Qobuz track links do.

With no errors in 41 accepted matches, plus 4 checked by hand, the error rate is plausibly below about 7% (the rule of three, 3/45). That is a sample bound. TON-16's Apple and TON-99's Qobuz results point the same way.

## Decisions (20 October 2026)

The creator decided, during the spike:

1. **Tidal is a launch service:** capture, matching, service links and opening, like the other three.
2. **Identification:** MusicBrainz's URL lookup, then transient hints from tidal.com's album page under Qobuz's rules ([G1 terms review](2026-10-19-g1-terms-review.md), decision 4). One request per pasted link, nothing kept, and manual entry isn't prefilled from the page.
3. **Tidal's API isn't used.** The creator asked for its guidelines to be checked in full before deciding. The result is below.
4. **Tidal track links** are accepted, explained, and sent to manual entry with the link kept as the album's Tidal link (FR-02.3's fallback).

## Link forms

Tidal's share menu gives `https://tidal.com/browse/album/{id}` and `https://tidal.com/browse/track/{id}`, sometimes with `?u` or a trailing `/u`. The web player uses `listen.tidal.com/album/{id}`, and newer links drop `/browse`. Ids are numeric.

- **The canonical form** is `https://tidal.com/album/{id}`, or `/track/{id}`. That is the form MusicBrainz stores; it cleans Tidal URLs to it.
  - `listen.tidal.com/…` and `www.tidal.com/…` weren't found in MusicBrainz, even for an album it links.
  - So step 2 asks for the canonical form, with the `browse`, `www.` and `listen.` forms in the same request. Only the canonical form ever matched.
- **Playlists, artists and videos** stay unsupported, as for the other services.
- The spike's parser is `tooling/src/spikes/g1-musicbrainz/links.ts`; the production parser is TON-39.

## Step 2: MusicBrainz's URL lookup

There are 75 Tidal album links in [`tooling/fixtures/g1-tidal/`](../../tooling/fixtures/g1-tidal/), drawn from Wikidata by [`sample.ts`](../../tooling/src/spikes/g1-tidal/sample.ts), which records Tidal album ids as P4577:

| Sample | Links | Known by URL | Right album |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wikidata, MusicBrainz release group known (P436) | 50 | 23 (46%) | 23 of 23 |
| Wikidata, no MusicBrainz link | 25 | 1 (4%) | — |

- **The right album every time.** Each album step 2 found is the release group Wikidata names.
- **Half is about Apple's rate.** In TON-14, step 2 found 53% of Apple links and, with the store forms, 67% of Qobuz links of albums MusicBrainz has.
- **Enrichment:** the 24 identified releases all had an original year and a barcode in MusicBrainz, and 23 had a Cover Art Archive front.
- **Wikidata favors notable albums**, so real collections may do worse. There are no creator's Tidal links yet; a few would be the real test.

## Tidal's terms

The [Developer Terms](https://developer.tidal.com/documentation/guidelines/guidelines-developer-terms), [Developer Guidelines](https://developer.tidal.com/documentation/guidelines/guidelines-developer-guidelines) and [Design Guidelines](https://developer.tidal.com/documentation/guidelines/guidelines-design-guidelines), all version 3.0 of 6 May 2024, were read in full, with their changelog and the [API reference](https://tidal-music.github.io/tidal-api-reference/) (1.10.188).

**What the API could do.** With client credentials, `GET /albums/{id}` returns the album's `barcodeId`, and `/tracks/{id}/relationships/albums` gives a track's album. That is exactly what Spotify's bridge does (TS-41 step 3).

**Why it can't be used.**

| Tonaris would | Tidal says | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send the album's barcode to MusicBrainz, the whole point of the bridge | Guidelines III prohibits, without express written approval, "Enabling the transfer of data to another service" | Breached by design |
| Use Tidal data beside MusicBrainz, Spotify, Apple and Qobuz | Guidelines III: "Integrating TIDAL content with content or streams from another service" | Breached by design |
| Let agents add albums by link through MCP (FR-10) | Terms II: no "offering that utilizes TIDAL content for any purpose in connection with any artificial intelligence or machine intelligence technologies". Added on purpose in Guidelines 2.0 (January 2024) and restated in Terms 3.0. | Breached, even though Tidal's values never reach an agent |
| Name services in plain text, without logos | Guidelines II: "TIDAL branding together with a link back". Design Guidelines: content attributed "with our logo", and none of it "next to content from similar services" | Conflicts with the design spec |
| Stay non-commercial and store nothing | Terms I licenses non-commercial apps only; Terms II allows "temporary caching of metadata and cover art" | Passes |

- **This is stricter than Spotify's terms**, whose risk the creator accepted (G1 terms review).
  - Spotify bans ingesting its content into AI models; Tidal bans any connection with AI.
  - Tidal lifts development-mode quotas only after a review of the app's source code, and its approval covers "the specific use case presented".
- **The API would also undermine the page hints.** Registering means accepting the Developer Terms, which forbid "any text or data mining or scraping in relation to the TIDAL Platform".
  - Without registering, reading one public album page per pasted link falls only under Tidal's consumer terms, as with Qobuz.
  - After registering, it would be a direct contract breach. So the API and the page hints exclude each other.
- **The only compliant route to the API** is Tidal's written approval, which Guidelines III allows for. The creator chose not to pursue it for now.

**The pages.**
- [robots.txt](https://tidal.com/robots.txt) allows everything for `*` and blocks AI-training crawlers by name, among them GPTBot and ClaudeBot.
- It reserves text and data mining rights for AI training (`/.well-known/tdmrep.json`), and album pages carry `noai, noimageai`.
- The consumer [terms](https://tidal.com/terms) ban using Tidal content "for any purpose in connection with artificial intelligence or machine intelligence technologies or services". Their page refused automated fetches (403), so their scraping wording is quoted only from search results.
- The reading is the one the G1 terms review made for Qobuz:
  - one page per pasted link is an insubstantial, transient use, which the EU Database Directive allows a lawful user (art. 8(1)); Tidal's EU entity is TIDAL Music AS, in Norway, under the EEA;
  - the facts go only into a MusicBrainz search, and nothing from Tidal is kept or reaches an agent.

  The AI clause is the residual risk: Tonaris has MCP, even though Tidal's facts never reach it.

## Step 4: Tidal's album pages

A Tidal album page carries a schema.org `MusicAlbum` block: `name`, `byArtist` (a list) and `datePublished`. Unlike Qobuz's, it names the artists, and the title keeps any edition note, since there is no separate version field. The harness is TON-99's: [`tidal.ts`](../../tooling/src/spikes/g1-tidal/tidal.ts) is a second store-page client for it, and its rules and report are reused.

| Pages | Links |
| --- | --- |
| Gave title, artist and date | 66 of 75 (88%) |
| Answered 404 | 9 |
| Dated 1 January (probably the year only) | 9 of 66 (14%) |
| Dated the 1st of another month | 7 of 66 (11%) |
| The year is the album's first year, where the search found the right album | 41 of 44 (93%) |

| Rule (TON-99's, each changing one criterion) | Accepted | Wrong | Recall | Recall with hints | Several albums |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clean titles, artist credit, release year, one album | 33 | 0 | 65% | 72% | 4 |
| …release year or the album's original year | 34 | 0 | 67% | 74% | 4 |
| …loose titles and artist names (TON-16's rule) | 38 | 0 | 75% | 83% | 4 |
| **…official albums and EPs only** (recommended) | **41** | **0** | **80%** | **89%** | **1** |
| …without the year | 40 | 0 | 78% | 87% | 4 |

TON-99's "version note" rule changes nothing for Tidal, which has no separate version, so it is left out.

- **Among the labelled links step 2 misses,** the recommended rule adds 21 of 27 (78%), 21 of the 24 with hints (88%).
- **The restriction to official albums and EPs** found 3 more right albums than TON-16's rule, and cut the ambiguous results from 4 to 1.

**What fails.** These are the 5 labelled links with hints that the recommended rule doesn't identify.
- *The Last in Line* (Dio): two official release groups pass, so it goes to confirmation, as it should.
- *Bad Mood Rising (Standard)*: TON-16's title cleaning doesn't count "(Standard)" as an edition note, so the search finds nothing. Adding "standard" to its edition words would fix it.
- *Explicit Lyrics (Edition Deluxe)*: the French word order is cleaned, but no release of the album passes the year: the deluxe edition is dated 2003.
- *Superfly*: the right album is in the search, but none of its releases has the page's year.
- *Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag* (soundtrack): Tidal credits the composer and the game, and the search finds nothing.

**The 9 pages that answered 404** are probably albums Tidal doesn't offer in Finland, where the spike ran, or no longer offers: a Tidal link carries no region, so the page answers for the visitor's. A pasted link comes from a store that sells the album, so *recall with hints* is the better estimate for real use. In production the visitor is the server, whose region may not be the user's (TON-25).

## Cost

- **One request per link** to tidal.com, `/album/{id}`. No slug is needed, so there's no second request as with Qobuz.
- **Its time:** p50 613 ms, p95 724 ms, outside MusicBrainz's queue. That is slower than Qobuz's 82 ms.
- **No throttling** in 75 page requests 2 s apart, plus robots.txt.
- **MusicBrainz:** two requests per link that reaches step 4, as for Apple and Qobuz, with a title-only search for 16 of 75.

## What this changes elsewhere

- **PRD:** Tidal is a launch service (FR-02.1, section 2), with G1's decisions, AC-06's track-link exception and a change-log entry.
- **Design spec:** Tidal is named in the plain-text service list and in the capture copy.
- **TS-40:** Tidal's link forms.
- **TS-41:**
  - step 2 applies to Tidal album links;
  - Tidal's page hints are a step 4, with Qobuz's rules;
  - Tidal's evidence and API verdict;
  - the open questions for TON-25.
- **TS-43:** opening Tidal links is unverified: the universal links on mobile, and whether a desktop scheme exists.
- **The M3 issues** take Tidal: TON-39 (parsing), TON-42 (the chain), TON-46 (service links) and TON-47 (opening). `streamingServices` in `packages/db` needs `tidal`.
- **Shared harness:** TON-99's store-page harness now runs Qobuz or Tidal (`--service tidal`), and TON-14's chain parses Tidal links. Their results are unchanged.
