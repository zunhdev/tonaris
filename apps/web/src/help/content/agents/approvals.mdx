Some changes an agent can't make alone, such as deleting folders, lists or albums. The agent asks, and Tonaris shows you exactly what would change. Nothing changes until you approve.

## How a request reaches you

- **In the chat.** The agent gives you a link to the request. Open it on any device where you use Tonaris, your phone included. If you aren't signed in there, Tonaris asks you to sign in first, then shows the request.
- **In the sidebar.** On a computer, **Approvals** shows in the sidebar while requests wait, with how many. It opens **Settings**, then **Connections**, where **Waiting for your approval** lists each request. Choose **Review** to open one.

Tonaris looks for new requests every 30 seconds, and when you come back to its window.

<Note kind="phone">
  A phone has no sidebar. Open the agent's link, or tap your account button at the top right, then
  **Settings**, then **Connections**.
</Note>

- Which AI app asks, and what it wants to do, such as "Move 2 albums to Trash".
- The parts of the request. Choose a part to open it: the folders and lists, the albums with the lists each one leaves, a field and the albums that use it, and **Who no longer sees it** when something you share would go.
- What follows, such as how long things stay in Trash.
- How long the request has left: **Expires in**, then minutes and seconds.

## Approve or decline

1. Read the request, and open its parts to check them.
2. Choose **Approve** to let the agent do it, or **Decline** to change nothing.
3. After you approve, go back to the chat. The agent can now make the change, once, exactly as you saw it.

If something changes before the agent acts, such as an album joining another list, Tonaris doesn't make the change. The agent asks again, with the new details.

A request expires after 30 minutes, and an expired request changes nothing. Ask the agent again if you still want it. Opening the page again later shows what you decided.

When a link could be more than one album, the request shows the album the agent chose. To pick another, decline, and tell the agent which one you mean.

What an agent deletes goes to Trash. You can [get it back for 30 days](/help/trash/get-it-back).

## See and remove connections

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />.
2. Choose **Connections**.

<Picture
  name="agents-connected-clients"
  alt="Connected clients in Settings: Claude with Manage collection and ChatGPT with Read-only, each with the day it connected, when it was last used, and a Revoke button."
/>

**Connected clients** lists each AI app, newest first, with its mode, the day it connected and when it was last used.

To disconnect an app, choose **Revoke**. It loses access the next time it tries to use Tonaris, and its requests still waiting go with it. To give it another mode, revoke it, then [connect it again](/help/agents/connect).
