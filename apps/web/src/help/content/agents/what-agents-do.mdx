An AI agent is an AI app, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, that you connect to Tonaris. You ask it about your collection in a chat, in your own words, and it reads or changes the collection for you. It works with your own account in that AI app: Tonaris has no chat of its own.

## How it works

Tonaris and the AI app talk through MCP, a common way for AI apps to use other services. You connect once and choose what the agent may do. The connection stays until you remove it.

A connection covers your whole collection. You can't limit it to one folder or list.

## What you can ask

For example:

- "Which albums in Late night came out before 1970?"
- "Make a list called Live favorites with every live album I have."
- "Add these links to To listen," followed by a few album links.
- "Set Format to Vinyl for every album in Hard bop."
- "Which albums are in Unsorted, and which of my lists would suit them?"
- "Give the folder Jazz a blue color and a saxophone icon."

Agents add albums from links only. Tonaris doesn't look for albums in the streaming services, so the agent finds a link itself or asks you for one.

Every change an agent makes follows the same rules as yours, and shows in Tonaris as it happens.

## Read-only or Manage collection

When you connect, you choose one of two modes:

| Mode                  | What the agent can do                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Read-only**         | Read and search your collection, Trash, custom fields and what others share with you. It can't change anything.                                              |
| **Manage collection** | Also add, edit, move and organize: make folders and lists, add albums from links, fix details, set fields, colors and icons, and get things back from Trash. |

**Read-only** is chosen for you unless you pick the other. To change the mode later, remove the connection and connect again.

## What always waits for you

Even with **Manage collection**, these wait for your approval in Tonaris:

- deleting folders, lists or albums, which go to Trash;
- removing an option that albums use from a choice field;
- deleting a custom field;
- adding an album when a link could be more than one album;
- copying a folder or list someone shares with you into your collection.

The agent gives you a link, and you see exactly what would change before you choose. [Approve requests and manage connections](/help/agents/approvals) shows how.

## What agents can never do

These stay in the Tonaris app, whatever the mode:

- delete anything from Trash for good;
- share, change who sees something, or stop sharing;
- change your account: your name, profile, sign-in email or passkeys;
- download your data;
- see or remove connections.

## Shared folders and lists

Your agent reads what others share with you, as you see it in **Shared with me**. With **Manage collection**, it can change a folder or list you can edit, as you could yourself. It can't change the owner's custom fields, or get anything back from the owner's Trash. See [Editing someone else's folder or list](/help/share/editing-a-share).

It works the other way too. When you share with someone as **Can edit**, their agent can change what you shared, in the same way.
