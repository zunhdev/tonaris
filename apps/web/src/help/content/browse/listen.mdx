Each album keeps one link for each service it's on. You choose the service every time: Tonaris never picks one for you, even when an album has only one link.

## From a card or a row

Each card and row names the album's services, such as **Spotify** or **Tidal**. Choose one to open the album there. The album panel doesn't open.

A card shows the services that fit after the year, then **+2** or similar for the rest. Choose it to see them all.

A row shows its services when the view has room. When it's narrow, such as with the album panel open, point at the row and choose the ↗ button at its end.

## Open in…

1. Move to an album with the arrow keys, or open it in the album panel.
2. Press <Keys k="O" />. **Open in…** lists every service the album has a link for.
3. Choose one, or press <Keys k="Enter" /> on it.

If the album has no links, Tonaris says so. To add one, see [Fix an album's details, cover and links](/help/add-albums/fix-details).

The **Links** section of the [album panel](/help/browse/album-panel) opens a service too.

## Web page or app

At first, links open the service's web page in a new tab. To open the apps instead:

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />.
2. In **Preferences**, under **Links**, set **Open links in** to **App**.

**App** works for Spotify, Apple Music, Qobuz and Tidal, on every device you use. If a service's app isn't installed, its links won't open. YouTube Music has no app for computers, so its links always open its web page.

## Your services, in your order

In **Settings**, then **Preferences**, **Streaming services** lists the five services, each with a switch.

- **Put the ones you use most first.** Drag a service by its handle, or select its switch and press <Keys k="⌥ ↑" /> or <Keys k="⌥ ↓" />. Cards, rows, **Open in…** and the album panel all list an album's links in this order.
- **Turn off the ones you don't use.** The album panel and **New album** then show link fields only for the services that are on. Links you've already saved still show, and you can still paste a link from any service.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, tap a service under a card, or the ↗ at the end of a row for **Open in…**. An album
  you open shows its links too. With **Web page** chosen, your phone may still open the service's
  app if you have it.
</Note>
