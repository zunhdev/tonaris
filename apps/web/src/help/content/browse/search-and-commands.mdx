Search opens over whatever you're doing, finds what you type as you type it, and opens what you choose.

## Open search

Choose **Search** at the top of the sidebar, or press <Keys k="⌘ K" />. The key works even while you're typing in a field. To close search, press <Keys k="Esc" />, or <Keys k="⌘ K" /> again.

## What it finds

- The names of your folders and lists.
- Album titles and artists.
- Folders, lists and albums people share with you, under **Shared with me**.

It doesn't look in descriptions, custom fields or Trash.

Results come in groups: **Folders**, **Lists**, **Albums**, **Shared with me** and **Commands**. Each result shows where it is, such as Moods › Late night, or **Unsorted**. A group shows 8 results at first; choose **Show all** for the rest.

Your own collection is searched on your device, so search works offline. What's shared with you needs a connection.

## How it matches

- **Part of a word is enough:** "lind" finds Ayo Lindqvist Quartet.
- **Capitals and accents don't matter:** "lucia" finds Lúcia Brandão, and "koen" finds Kōen no Asa.
- **Spelling mistakes aren't forgiven:** "lindquist" finds nothing.
- **Other alphabets can be typed in Latin letters:** "kino" finds Кино, "aerzte" finds Die Ärzte, and "sonder" or "soender" finds Kai Sønder. Chinese, Japanese and Korean names match only as written.

Names that start with what you typed come first, then names with a word that starts with it.

## Open what it finds

1. Type a few letters.
2. Move to a result with <Keys k="↓" /> and <Keys k="↑" />, or point at it.
3. Press <Keys k="Enter" />, or choose it.

A folder or list opens. An album opens in its list, with the [album panel](/help/browse/album-panel). To open an album on a page of its own instead, press <Keys k="⌘ Enter" />. Something shared with you opens in its shared view.

## Run a command

Every action that has a key is also a command here, and some actions are only here, such as **Move to position…**, **Color…** and **Icon…**.

1. Type part of the command's name, such as _new list_. To see only commands, type **>** first.
2. Press <Keys k="Enter" />, or choose it.

Each command shows its key, so you learn the keys as you go. A command acts on what had the focus before you opened search: a folder or list in the sidebar, an album in the view, or the album in the panel. A command that can't run is dimmed and says why, such as "Open or focus an album first".

## Before you type

An empty search shows:

- **Recent:** the last 8 folders, lists and albums you opened on this device.
- **Frequent commands:** the 5 commands you run most from here. At first, these are **Add albums from links**, **New list**, **Go to Unsorted**, **Switch to editing** and **Keyboard shortcuts**.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, tap the magnifying glass at the top. Search fills the screen and finds the same
  things. Its commands go places, open View options and open **Open in…**; changes need a computer.
</Note>
