<Keys k="Tab" /> moves forward through the screen, and <Keys k="⇧ Tab" /> back. The sidebar's
folders and lists take one Tab stop, and so does a view's grid of albums; inside them, the arrow
keys move.

## When keys wait

While you type in a field, keys type: a G is a G. Three keys still work there: <Keys k="⌘ K" /> opens search, <Keys k="Esc" /> closes or cancels, and <Keys k="⌘ Enter" /> saves a form. Keys also wait while a dialog or menu is open, which has keys of its own.

## The sidebar

- <Keys k="↑" /> and <Keys k="↓" /> move between folders and lists. <Keys k="Home" /> and
  <Keys k="End" /> go to the first and last.
- <Keys k="→" /> opens a folder, then moves into it. <Keys k="←" /> closes it, or goes to the folder
  above.
- <Keys k="Enter" /> opens the folder or list in the view.
- Type the first letters of a name to jump to it. Letters that are keys of their own, such as N or M, do their own job instead.
- <Keys k="⇧ F10" /> opens a folder's or list's menu, as its **⋯** button does.
- <Keys k="N" /> makes a new list there, <Keys k="⇧ N" /> a new folder. <Keys k="F2" /> renames the
  folder or list, and <Keys k="M" /> moves it.

## Going places

- <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="H" />: Collection, the top of your collection.
- <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="U" />: Unsorted.
- <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="T" />: Trash.
- <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />: Settings.
- <Keys k="[" />: hide the sidebar for more room, or show it again.

Press the second key within a second and a half.

## In a view

A view's albums, or its folders and lists, work as one grid, rows or covers alike:

- <Keys k="↓" /> or <Keys k="J" />: the next row. <Keys k="↑" /> or <Keys k="K" />: the previous
  row.
- <Keys k="←" /> and <Keys k="→" />: along a row.
- <Keys k="⌘ Home" /> and <Keys k="⌘ End" />: the first and last item.
- <Keys k="Enter" />: open a folder or list, or an album in the album panel.
- <Keys k="V" />: [View options](/help/browse/covers-or-rows). <Keys k="E" />: switch between
  Viewing and Editing.
- <Keys k="⌥ ↑" /> and <Keys k="⌥ ↓" />: move the item up or down, in [manual
  order](/help/organize/move-and-rearrange).

## Selecting albums

In a list or Unsorted:

- <Keys k="X" /> selects the album, or deselects it. This switches to Editing.
- <Keys k="⇧ ↓" /> and <Keys k="⇧ ↑" /> add the album below or above.
- <Keys k="Esc" /> clears the selection.

Then <Keys k="L" /> adds them all to a list, <Keys k="⌫" /> takes them out of this list, and <Keys k="⌘ ⌫" /> deletes them, after asking.

## With an album open

The focus stays in the view, so the album panel follows it:

- <Keys k="J" /> or <Keys k="K" />, or the arrow keys: the next or previous album.
- <Keys k="O" />: **Open in…**, to [listen](/help/browse/listen).
- <Keys k="L" />: add the album to a list.
- <Keys k="Esc" />: close the panel.

## Dialogs, menus and messages

- <Keys k="Tab" /> moves between a dialog's fields and buttons.
- <Keys k="⌘ Enter" /> saves a form such as **Add albums** or **New album**.
- <Keys k="Esc" /> closes a dialog or menu.
- In a menu, or a list of choices such as **Add to list…**, <Keys k="↑" /> and <Keys k="↓" /> move, and <Keys k="Enter" /> chooses.
- <Keys k="F6" /> reaches a message in the bottom-right corner, such as one offering **Undo**.

## Search and commands

<Keys k="⌘ K" /> opens search from anywhere. Every action with a key, such as **New list**, is also
a command there, with its key beside it. See [Search and
commands](/help/browse/search-and-commands).

## Every key

Press <Keys k="?" />, or choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Keyboard shortcuts**. The same list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).
