The album panel shows everything about one album. It opens beside the list, so you can keep looking through it. On a narrow window, it opens over the list instead.

## Open and close it

1. Choose an album's row or card. Or move to it with the arrow keys and press <Keys k="Enter" />.
2. To see the next album, press <Keys k="J" />, and the previous one, <Keys k="K" />. The arrow keys work too.
3. To close it, choose **×** at its top right, or press <Keys k="Esc" />.

The album is part of the page's address, so it stays open after a reload.

## What it shows

From the top:

- **The cover, title and artist.** The top of the panel takes the cover's color.
- **Links:** each streaming service the album has a link for. Choose one to [listen there](/help/browse/listen).
- **Custom fields:** your own fields and their values, such as a rating or where you bought it.
- **Lists:** every list the album is in. Choose one to open it. An album in no list shows **Unsorted**.
- **Properties:** its release type, whether it's live, its year and a live album's recording years, when you added it, how many lists it's in, and its services. A line under them says which year it sorts by.

## Make it wider or narrower

Drag the panel's left edge. Or press <Keys k="Tab" /> until the edge is highlighted, then <Keys k="←" /> to widen the panel or <Keys k="→" /> to narrow it. <Keys k="Home" /> makes it narrowest, <Keys k="End" /> widest. Tonaris remembers the width on this device.

## An album on a page of its own

Search for the album with <Keys k="⌘ K" />, then press <Keys k="⌘ Enter" /> on it. Its page shows the same details, with **View** and **Edit** at the top. More in [Search and commands](/help/browse/search-and-commands).

## Edit in the panel

Switch to Editing: choose **Edit** at the top of the view, or press <Keys k="E" />. The panel's details become fields:

- the cover's address, with **Find** to look for a cover;
- the title and artist;
- **Chronology:** the release type, **Live**, and the years;
- each link, with **Replace** and **Remove**, or **Add Spotify link** and the like;
- custom fields, with **Add field**;
- lists: **×** on a list takes the album out of it, and **Add to list**, or <Keys k="L" />, puts it in another.

A field saves when you press <Keys k="Enter" /> or leave it. <Keys k="Esc" /> puts back what was there. Every list the album is in shows the change. [Fix an album's details](/help/add-albums/fix-details) explains each one.

## Delete the album

In Editing mode, choose the trash icon at the top of the panel, **Delete from collection…**. Or press <Keys k="⌘ ⌫" />. Tonaris asks first, then moves the album to Trash, where it stays for 30 days. [Remove, delete and undo](/help/trash/remove-delete-undo).

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, tap an album to open it in a sheet from the bottom of the screen. It shows the same
  details, without editing. Tap a link to listen, or **×** to close it.
</Note>
