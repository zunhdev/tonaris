Open a folder, a list or **Unsorted**, then choose **View**, the button with sliders at the right end of its header. Or press <Keys k="V" />. Your choices change this view only, and Tonaris remembers them.

## Covers or rows

Under **Layout**:

- **Rows** shows one album a line: its cover, title, artist, year and release type, and its services when there's room. More albums fit on the screen.
- **Grid** shows each album as a card with a large cover, then its title, artist, year and services. A cover that isn't a plain studio album is labeled EP, Single, Compilation or Live.

Folders have both layouts too. In **Grid**, each folder or list is a card fanning the first three covers inside it. In **Rows**, each shows its name, the first line of its description, and how much it holds.

## Sort lists and Unsorted

Under **Sort**:

- **Year**: by the year each album shows.
- **Name**: by album title. Capitals and accents don't change the order.
- **Manual**: your own order. Until you change it, a list keeps the order you added albums in, and Unsorted shows the newest first. [Move albums into your own order](/help/organize/move-and-rearrange).

**Direction** is **Ascending** (oldest first, or A to Z) or **Descending**. Manual order has no direction.

## What the year means

The year is when the album first came out, never a reissue's year. A live album shows when it was recorded instead, after "rec.", such as rec. 1998. If the recording year isn't known, it shows the release year with a small calendar mark. Sorting by **Year** uses the year you see. [Where the year comes from](/help/add-albums/where-details-come-from).

## Group by release type

In a list or Unsorted, set **Grouping** to **Release type**. Albums then come under headers, in this order: Album, EP, Single, Live, Compilation, Unclassified. A live album is always under Live. Each group keeps the sort you chose, and changing the sort never changes the grouping.

To fold a group away, choose its header, or press <Keys k="Enter" /> on it. It stays folded until you close the browser tab.

## Sort folders

A folder, and **Collection** at the top, sort what they hold by **Name**, by **Created** (the date each was made, shown beside it in **Rows**), or **Manual**. With **Name** and **Created**, folders come first, then lists. The sidebar always shows a folder's contents in the same order as its view.

## Each view remembers

Each view keeps its own choices, saved to your account, so they follow you to your other devices. Sorting one list by **Name** leaves your other lists as they were.

At the bottom of View options:

- **Use account defaults** forgets this view's choices.
- **Save as my default** makes them the default for every view of its kind.

## Defaults for new views

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />.
2. In **Preferences**, set **Lists and Unsorted** and **Folders and Collection**.

Views where you've made choices keep them. At first, lists and Unsorted show rows, sorted by year, oldest first, and folders show cards in manual order.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, tap the sliders icon at the top for the same choices. Manual order shows as you saved
  it; changing it needs a computer.
</Note>
