The feedback board is where you share ideas, report problems, and see what's planned. You sign in to it with your Tonaris account.

## Open the feedback board

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap your account button at the top right.
2. Choose **Feedback and ideas**.

  name="help-account-menu"
  alt="The account menu open at the top of the sidebar, with Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, Feedback and ideas, Terms of use, Privacy notice and Sign out."
  caption="Feedback and ideas opens the board in a new tab."
/>

The board opens in a new tab, at feedback.tonaris.app, and you arrive signed in. You need no other account.

## How the board works

- **It's public.** Anyone can read it, but only Tonaris members can post, vote and comment.
- **Vote before you post.** Look for a post like yours first, and vote for it. Votes show what matters to the most people.
- **Your name, not your email.** The board shows your display name with what you post. It gets your email address too, to tell you about replies, but never shows it.
- **See what's coming.** When a request is taken up, its post is marked **Planned**, **Started** or **Completed**.
- **Posts stay.** What you post stays on the board if you delete your Tonaris account.

## Report a problem

A clear report helps fix a problem sooner. Say:

- what you did, step by step;
- what you expected, and what happened instead;
- any message Tonaris showed, word for word;
- your device and browser, such as "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows, Chrome".

A screenshot helps too.

<Note kind="info">
  The board is public. Leave out your email address, sign-in codes, and anything else private.
</Note>

If a change didn't save, [When an edit doesn't save](/help/saving/when-an-edit-doesnt-save) may already have the answer. Many questions are answered in [Questions and answers](/help/help/questions).

## Problems with your account

For a problem you shouldn't post in public, such as losing access to your sign-in email, write to the support address on [Lost access to your email?](/help/recovery). Write from the address you use now, and say which address you sign in with.
