## Is there a password?

No. Tonaris has no passwords. You sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email, or with a passkey: your face, fingerprint or screen lock. [Your sign-in email and passkeys](/help/account/email-and-passkeys)

## What if my sign-in code doesn't arrive?

Look in your spam or junk folder, and check the email address you typed. After 30 seconds, you can ask for a new code; each code works for 10 minutes. [Joining and signing in](/help/start-here/joining-and-signing-in)

If you can't get email at that address any more, see [Lost access to your email?](/help/recovery)

## Does Tonaris play music?

No. Tonaris keeps your albums and opens each one in the service you listen with: Spotify, Apple Music, Qobuz, Tidal or YouTube Music. Choose a service's name on an album, or press <Keys k="O" /> for **Open in…**. [Listen in your streaming service](/help/browse/listen)

## Can I import my Spotify library?

No. Tonaris doesn't connect to your streaming libraries, in either direction, and importing isn't planned. Instead, copy album links from your service and paste them into **Add albums**, up to <Limit of="batch" /> at once and <Limit of="capturesPerHour" /> in an hour. An AI agent can also add albums from links. [Add albums by pasting links](/help/add-albums/paste-links)

## Can I add songs or playlists?

Tonaris keeps albums, EPs, singles, live albums and compilations, not songs. A link to a song adds the album the song is on. Playlist links don't work: to keep a playlist's albums, make a list and add each album's link. [Services and links Tonaris reads](/help/reference/services-and-links)

## Why does an album show this year?

Tonaris shows the year the album first came out, not the year of the edition your link points to: a 2015 remaster of a 1972 album shows 1972. A live album shows the year it was recorded, after **rec.**, when that's known. You can change the year in the album panel. [Where album details come from](/help/add-albums/where-details-come-from)

## Why is a cover missing?

Some albums have no cover where Tonaris looks them up. To add one, select the album and switch to Editing with <Keys k="E" />. Then choose **Find** beside **Artwork URL** to pick a cover, or paste an image's address there. [Fix an album's details, cover and links](/help/add-albums/fix-details)

## Can I keep a vinyl and a CD edition apart?

Not as two albums. Tonaris keeps each album once, whichever edition a link points to, so a link to a deluxe edition adds the same album. To note what you own, add a custom field, such as a multiple-choice **Format** with Vinyl, CD and Cassette. [Track anything with custom fields](/help/fields/custom-fields)

## What is Unsorted?

**Unsorted** holds the albums that are in no list. An album you add without a list goes there, and so does one you take out of its last list. Put it in a list, and it leaves Unsorted. [One album, many lists, and Unsorted](/help/organize/one-album-many-lists)

## How many albums can I keep?

## Who can see my collection?

Only you, until you share. When you share a folder or list, the people you invite see it, and so does anyone with its link if you make one. AI agents you connect see your whole collection. The people you share with also see your profile. [Ways to share](/help/share/ways-to-share)

## Can someone else change my list?

Only if you share it with them as **Can edit**. They can then add, change and arrange what's in it, and what they delete goes to your Trash. People with **Can view**, and anyone with its link, can only look. [Share a folder or list](/help/share/share-a-folder-or-list)

## Can an AI agent delete my albums?

Not without you. An agent connected as **Read-only** can't change anything. With **Manage collection**, deleting waits until you approve it in Tonaris, and what's deleted stays in Trash for 30 days. [Approve requests and manage connections](/help/agents/approvals)

## Can I get back something deleted more than 30 days ago?

No. After 30 days in Trash, a folder, list or album is deleted for good, and nobody can bring it back. To keep a copy of everything, download your data now and then. [Get something back from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back)

## Can I take my data with me?

Yes. On a computer, open **Settings**, then **Data**, and choose **Download your data**. You get a ZIP file with your whole collection, Trash included, readable by people and by programs. Tonaris can't read an export back in. [Export your collection](/help/account/export)

## Can I use Tonaris on my phone?

Yes, in Safari or Chrome, and you can add it to your Home Screen. On a phone, you browse, search and open albums; adding and organizing need a computer. [Tonaris on your phone](/help/phone/on-your-phone)

## Does Tonaris work without an internet connection?

No. If your connection drops, Tonaris keeps showing what it last loaded, and editing waits until you're back online. [How saving works](/help/saving/how-saving-works)

## Is Tonaris in my language?

Tonaris itself is in English only for now. Your albums, lists and notes can be in any language, and keep their own spelling. Search finds names in other alphabets by their Latin spelling too: _kino_ finds _Кино_. [Search and commands](/help/browse/search-and-commands)

## Is there a light theme?

Not yet: Tonaris has a dark theme only. You can ask for a light theme, or vote for it, on the feedback board. [Get in touch](/help/help/get-in-touch)
