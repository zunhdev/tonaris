Enter an album yourself when it isn't on a streaming service, or when Tonaris can't find it from its link. Tonaris saves exactly what you type.

## Open the form

1. Choose **Add album** in the sidebar, or press <Keys k="C" />.
2. Choose **Enter manually**. The **New album** form opens.

## What you must fill in

- **Title** and **Artist**.
- **Original release year**: the year the album first came out, as four digits, such as 1986.

For a live album, switch on **Live**. A **Recording year or range** field appears: type the year it was recorded, or a range such as 1978–1982. A live album needs its recording year or its release year, so you can fill in either one, or both. Your lists then show the recording year after "rec.".

If a field is empty or can't be read, it says why, such as "Give the album an artist."

## What you can add

- **Release type** starts at **Album**. Choose **EP**, **Single**, **Compilation** or **Unknown** if it's something else.
- **Artwork URL**: an https link to a cover image. Or choose **Find** to pick a cover from the Cover Art Archive for the title and artist you typed.
- **Spotify link**, **Apple Music link** and the other link fields: paste an album or song link. Tonaris checks that it's a link for that service.
- **Custom fields**: choose **Add field…** to give the album a rating, a format, or any field you made. See [Track anything with custom fields](/help/fields/custom-fields).

The form has a link field for each service you use. To change which services those are, go to **Settings**, then **Preferences**, and turn services on or off under **Streaming services**. Press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" /> to go there.

## Choose its list

**List** starts with the list you're viewing, or with **No list (goes to Unsorted)**. To change it, choose **List**, type a few letters of a list's name, and choose the list.

## Save it

Choose **Add album**, or press <Keys k="⌘ Enter" />. The album appears in its list, or in **Unsorted**.

To stop, choose **Cancel** or press <Keys k="Esc" />. If you typed anything, Tonaris asks "Discard this album?" first.

<Note kind="info">
  Tonaris doesn't look up an album you enter yourself, so it won't recognize it from another
  service's link later. To give it more links, open it in the album panel and [add them
  there](/help/add-albums/fix-details#add-replace-or-remove-a-link). Adding a link with **Add
  album** may make a second copy.
</Note>
