An album's details are yours to change. An album is the same in every list it's in, so a fix shows everywhere. Changes need Editing mode: choose **Edit** at the top of the view, or press <Keys k="E" />.

## Edit in a row

When albums show as rows, you can change the title, artist and year in place.

1. Choose **Edit**, or press <Keys k="E" />.
2. Double-click the title, the artist or the year. Or move to it with the arrow keys and press <Keys k="Enter" />. For the title, <Keys k="F2" /> works too, and switches to Editing for you.
3. Type the new value and press <Keys k="Enter" />. Press <Keys k="Esc" /> to cancel.

A year has four digits, such as 1986. For a live album that shows "rec.", the year is its recording year: type a range such as 1978–1982 for several years. Title, artist and year can't be left empty.

## Edit in the album panel

1. In Editing mode, click the album to open it in the album panel.
2. Change the title and the artist, under the cover.
3. Under **Chronology**, choose the **Release type**, switch **Live** on or off, or change **Original release year** and, for a live album, **Recording year or range**.

Each field saves when you press <Keys k="Enter" /> or leave it. <Keys k="Esc" /> cancels.

## Change the cover

In Editing mode, under the cover in the album panel:

- Paste an https link to an image into **Artwork URL**, and press <Keys k="Enter" />.
- Or choose **Find** to see covers from the Cover Art Archive for the album's title and artist, and choose one.

Empty **Artwork URL** to remove the cover.

## Add, replace or remove a link

In Editing mode, **Links** has a row for each service you use, and for any link the album already has.

- **Add Spotify link**, or another service's, opens a field. Paste an album or song link and press <Keys k="Enter" />.
- **Replace** opens the same field, holding the current link.
- **Remove** takes the link off at once. Choose **Undo** in the message to put it back.

An album has one link per service. Tonaris checks each link: one it can't read, or one for another service, stays in the field with the reason, such as "This is an Apple Music link. Paste a Spotify link here."

To choose which services get a row, go to **Settings**, then **Preferences**, under **Streaming services**, or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />.

## Paste a link onto an open album

With an album open in the album panel and no text field selected, press <Keys k="⌘ V" />. Tonaris switches to Editing, puts each pasted link in its service's row, and says what it did, such as "Added the Tidal link", with **Undo**. A link replaces the album's link for that service. Paste one link per service at a time.

If Tonaris kept an album's link when you added another for the same service, the panel says "Another Spotify link was found for this album". Choose **Replace** to use the new link, or **Dismiss** to keep yours.

## If the album changed on another device

Changes made elsewhere show here as they happen, but never replace what you're typing. If you then save a value that changed elsewhere, your change isn't saved, and a message says the album changed while you were editing.

1. Choose **Review** in the message. Tonaris shows both versions.
2. Choose **Keep current**, or **Apply mine** to save yours over it.

See [When an edit doesn't save](/help/saving/when-an-edit-doesnt-save).
