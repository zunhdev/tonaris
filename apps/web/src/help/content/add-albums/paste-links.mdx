A link is the quickest way to add an album. It tells Tonaris which album you mean, and Tonaris fills in the title, artist, year, release type and cover.

## Which links work

Album and song links from the five services work, and Spotify's short links too. For a song, Tonaris adds the album the song is on.

Playlist, artist and video links don't. Tidal and Qobuz song links don't say which album they're on, so Tonaris asks you for the album's details. [Services and links Tonaris reads](/help/reference/services-and-links) lists every form.

## Copy a link

In your streaming app, open the album's share menu and copy its link. On a service's website, the address in your browser works too, except in YouTube Music: use its Share menu there.

## Add albums from links

1. Choose **Add album** in the sidebar, or press <Keys k="C" />.
2. Paste the links into **Links**, one per line.
3. Check **List**. It starts with the list you're viewing. Choose it to pick another list, or **No list (goes to Unsorted)**.
4. Choose **Add**, or press <Keys k="⌘ Enter" />.

You can add up to <Limit of="batch" /> links at once. In Editing mode, a list also has **Add album** below its albums.

## Paste straight into a list

Open a list and press <Keys k="⌘ V" />. Each pasted line that is a link becomes an album in that list. Tonaris switches to Editing for you.

This works while no text field is selected and no album is open. With an album open, the paste [adds the link to that album](/help/add-albums/fix-details#paste-a-link-onto-an-open-album) instead.

Paste anywhere else, such as in a folder, and the albums go to **Unsorted**, where albums in no list wait. A message offers **Add to list…**.

## While Tonaris works

Each link shows as a row at the top of the list it goes to, or of **Unsorted**, reading "Resolving link…". You can keep working. When the album is found, the row becomes the album, with [details from MusicBrainz](/help/add-albums/where-details-come-from).

With several links, a panel at the bottom right counts them: "Adding 12 · 8 done · 2 need you · 1 failed". Choose it to see each link's result in **Activity**.

## Albums you already have

An album is in your collection once. If a link is for an album you have, even another edition of it, Tonaris puts that album in the list instead of adding a copy: "Already in your collection — added to this list", or "Already in this list".

If the album has no link for that service yet, it gets this one. If it has a different one, Tonaris keeps yours: "Kept your existing Spotify link". Choose **Review** to replace it.

An album in Trash comes back: "Restored from Trash and added". It goes into the list you're adding to, or Unsorted, not the lists it was in before.

## When a link doesn't work

If Tonaris can't read a line, or can't reach MusicBrainz, the row stays with the reason and these choices:

- **Retry** tries again.
- **Enter manually** opens the album form to [add the album by hand](/help/add-albums/add-by-hand), with the link filled in when Tonaris can read it.
- **Dismiss** drops the link.

The row stays until you dismiss it, even after a reload. When a link fits several albums, or Tonaris needs details, it [asks you](/help/add-albums/choose-a-match).

## Limits

Tonaris adds up to <Limit of="capturesPerHour" /> albums from links an hour. Past that, the row says so: try later, or enter the album by hand. Links that failed or wait for you don't count. Your collection holds up to <Limit of="albums" /> albums, Trash included.
