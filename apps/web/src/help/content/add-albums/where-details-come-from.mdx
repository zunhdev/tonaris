A link only tells Tonaris which album you mean. The album's details come from two free, open sources that music fans keep up to date.

## MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive

- **MusicBrainz** is an open music encyclopedia that anyone can edit. Tonaris takes the album's title, artist, year and release type from it, and, for a live album, when it was recorded.
- **The Cover Art Archive** holds covers that MusicBrainz's community uploads. Tonaris uses the album's front cover, when there is one.

Tonaris doesn't copy details from the streaming services. One exception: when MusicBrainz doesn't have an album from an Apple Music link, the album form offers Apple's title, artist and year for you to check.

An album without a cover shows its first letter instead. You can [add a cover](/help/add-albums/fix-details#change-the-cover) yourself.

## Albums, not editions

Tonaris keeps albums, not editions. A remaster, a deluxe edition or a vinyl reissue is the same album as the original. Whichever edition your link is for, Tonaris saves the album with its original title and the year it first came out.

So each album is in your collection once. A link to another edition of an album you have goes to that album. Two different albums with the same title stay apart.

## Which year an album shows

- **Most albums** show the year they first came out, from their earliest release.
- **Live albums** show when they were recorded, after "rec.": rec. 1998, or rec. 1975–1985 for a recording made over several years.
- **A live album whose recording year isn't known** shows its release year, with a small calendar mark. Point to the mark to read "Recording year unknown — sorted by release year".

Sorting by year uses the year the album shows. Every album has a year: when MusicBrainz doesn't know it, Tonaris asks you for it.

## Release types

An album's release type is **Album**, **EP**, **Single** or **Compilation**, from MusicBrainz. **Live** is separate, so an album can be a live compilation. When MusicBrainz doesn't say, the type is **Unknown**.

When albums show as covers, a small label in a cover's top-left corner says EP, Single, Compilation or Live. A studio album has no label. In rows, the type has a column of its own.

## Your edits stay

Tonaris fills in only what's missing. It never changes a value an album already has, whether you typed it or it came from MusicBrainz. When you add a link for an album you already have, Tonaris may fill a gap, such as a missing cover, and leaves everything else as it is.

So if you change an album's year or type, it stays as you set it. MusicBrainz has no say over it after that.

## What Properties shows

Open an album and scroll to **Properties**, at the bottom of the album panel. It lists:

- **Release type** and **Live**.
- **Original release year**, and for a live album **Recording year or range**, with a line saying which year the album sorts by.
- **Added**: the date you added the album.
- **In lists**: how many lists it's in, or "None, in Unsorted".
- **Links**: the services it has links for.

In Editing mode, the release type and years move up, under **Chronology**, where you can change them.

<Note kind="phone">On a phone, open an album to see the same Properties.</Note>
