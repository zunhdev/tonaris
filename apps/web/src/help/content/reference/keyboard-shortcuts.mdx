On a computer, almost everything in Tonaris has a key. Here they all are, grouped by what they do: switch between Mac and Windows to see the keys your computer uses.

<Shortcuts />

## How the keys work

- **Keys wait while you type.** In a text field, and while a dialog or menu is open, the keys above do nothing, so you can type freely. Three work anyway: <Keys k="⌘ K" /> opens search, <Keys k="Esc" /> closes or cancels, and <Keys k="⌘ Enter" /> sends a form.
- **Two keys in a row.** For keys such as <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="H" />, press the first key, let go, then press the second within a second and a half.
- **See them in Tonaris.** Press <Keys k="?" />, or choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- **Every command is in search too.** Press <Keys k="⌘ K" /> and type a command's name, such as _Add to list_: it shows its key beside it. Type `>` first to see commands only. A few commands, such as **Color…**, **Icon…** and **Move to position…**, have no key, so you find them only there.
- **Phones have no keys.** On a phone, use the buttons at the top of each screen.

To move through the sidebar and your albums by keyboard alone, see [Move around with the keyboard](/help/browse/keyboard).
