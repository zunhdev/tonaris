The words below are the ones Tonaris shows on screen. Each one links to the page that says more.

## Your collection

**Collection**: everything you keep in Tonaris: your folders, lists and albums. It's at the top of the sidebar, and it's the first screen on a phone. Press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="H" /> to go there. [Your first ten minutes](/help/start-here/your-first-ten-minutes)

**Album**: one album, EP, single, live album or compilation, with its title, artist, year, cover and links. Each album is in your collection once, however many lists it's in. [Add albums by pasting links](/help/add-albums/paste-links)

**List**: a set of albums you put together, such as Late night or To listen. An album can be in as many lists as you like. [Put albums in lists and take them out](/help/organize/lists-in-and-out)

**Folder**: holds lists, and other folders, to keep them in order. A folder holds no albums of its own. [Folders and lists](/help/organize/folders-and-lists)

**Unsorted**: where albums that are in no list wait. Take an album out of its last list, and it goes there; nothing is deleted. Press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="U" />. [One album, many lists, and Unsorted](/help/organize/one-album-many-lists)

**Remove and delete**: removing takes an album out of a list, and it stays in your collection. Deleting moves it to Trash. [Remove, delete and undo](/help/trash/remove-delete-undo)

**Trash**: keeps what you delete for 30 days, so you can get it back. After that, it's gone for good. Press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="T" />. [Get something back from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back)

**Album panel**: the panel that opens on the right when you select an album, with its details, links and fields. On a phone, an album opens in a sheet from the bottom instead. [The album panel](/help/browse/album-panel)

**Viewing and Editing**: the two modes on a computer. Viewing is for looking and listening; Editing lets you rename, fix details and rearrange. Press <Keys k="E" /> to switch. [Viewing and Editing](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing)

**Custom field**: something you add to albums yourself, such as a rating, the format you own, or the day you bought it. [Track anything with custom fields](/help/fields/custom-fields)

**Release type**: what kind of release an album is. A list can be grouped by it: Album, EP, Single, Live, Compilation and Unclassified. [Covers or rows, sorting and grouping](/help/browse/covers-or-rows)

## Sharing

**Can view**: a person you share with as Can view sees the folder or list, and can't change it. [Share a folder or list](/help/share/share-a-folder-or-list)

**Can edit**: a person you share with as Can edit can also add albums, change them and arrange them. Your custom fields, sharing and Trash stay yours. [Editing someone else's folder or list](/help/share/editing-a-share)

**Link**: a folder or list shared by link opens for anyone who has the link, without an account. [Ways to share](/help/share/ways-to-share)

**Guest**: someone who opens a shared link without signing in. Guests can look and open albums, nothing more. [Change or stop sharing](/help/share/change-or-stop)

**Shared with me**: the place in the sidebar for the folders and lists others share with you. [When someone shares with you](/help/share/shared-with-you)

**People**: everyone you share with, and everyone who shares with you. [People](/help/share/people)

## AI agents

**Agent**: an AI app, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, that you let read or organize your collection. [What an AI agent can do for you](/help/agents/what-agents-do)

**Connection**: the link between Tonaris and one AI app, with what it may do: **Read-only** or **Manage collection**. [Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex](/help/agents/connect)

**Approval**: your answer to a change an agent can't make alone, such as deleting. [Approve requests and manage connections](/help/agents/approvals)

## Saving and your account

**Activity**: on a computer, the row at the bottom of the sidebar. It lists changes that are still saving or couldn't be saved, with what to do. [How saving works](/help/saving/how-saving-works)

**Export**: a download of your whole collection as one ZIP file, from **Settings**, then **Data**. [Export your collection](/help/account/export)

**Passkey**: lets your phone or computer sign you in with your face, fingerprint or screen lock, instead of a code by email. [Your sign-in email and passkeys](/help/account/email-and-passkeys)
