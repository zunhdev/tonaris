The limits are high, and most collections never reach one. They keep Tonaris quick for everyone.

## Your collection

| What                                                         | Limit                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Albums, Trash included                                       | <Limit of="albums" />          |
| Folders and lists together, Trash included                   | <Limit of="containers" />      |
| Levels of folders inside folders                             | 32                             |
| Places in lists: each time an album is in a list counts once | <Limit of="memberships" />     |
| Custom fields                                                | <Limit of="fields" />          |

An invitation you accept always fits in **Shared with me**, even past its limit.

## How much at a time

| What                                                        | Limit                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Albums added from links in one hour, by you or your agents  | <Limit of="capturesPerHour" /> |
| Downloads of your data in one hour                          | <Limit of="exportsPerHour" />  |
| Links pasted at once, or items in one request from an agent | <Limit of="batch" />           |

Trash keeps what you delete for 30 days.

## At a limit

Only the new thing is refused, with a message that says why and what to do. Everything else keeps working: you can still open, edit, move, remove and share.

- **At the album limit,** a new album is refused. Getting an album back from Trash still works, and so does adding a link to an album you already have, since neither needs a new place.
- **At the hourly limit on links,** you can still add albums by hand, or try again later.

What people add to a folder or list they can edit for you counts toward your limits, as it's in your collection.

## Trash counts

Albums, folders and lists in Trash count until they're deleted for good, by you or after 30 days. To make room now, [delete them from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back) on a computer. Taking an album out of a list frees nothing: the album is still in your collection.

## See your usage

In **Settings**, then **Data**, **Usage** shows how many albums you have of the limit, how many of them are in Trash, and a bar for both. To get there, choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />. Then choose **Data**.
