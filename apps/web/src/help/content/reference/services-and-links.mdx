Copy a link from a service's Share menu, and paste it into Tonaris. A link to an album adds that album. A link to a song adds the album the song is on.

## The five services

| Service           | Links that work                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Spotify**       | Album and song links from `open.spotify.com`, short links from `spotify.link`, and `spotify:album:` and `spotify:track:` codes |
| **Apple Music**   | Album and song links from `music.apple.com`                                                                                    |
| **Qobuz**         | Album pages on `qobuz.com`, and album and track links from `open.qobuz.com` and `play.qobuz.com`                               |
| **Tidal**         | Album and track links from `tidal.com` and `listen.tidal.com`                                                                  |
| **YouTube Music** | Album links from YouTube Music's Share menu, and songs played inside an album                                                  |

Links can have extra parts, such as a country or `?si=` at the end. Tonaris tidies each link before saving it, so two forms of the same link count as one.

## Songs from Tidal and Qobuz

Their song links don't say which album a song is on. So Tonaris asks you for the album's title, artist and year, and keeps the link with the album. See [Add an album by hand](/help/add-albums/add-by-hand).

## YouTube Music

On YouTube Music, an album is a special kind of playlist. To get a link that works:

1. In YouTube Music, open the album.
2. Copy its link from **Share**.

A link to a song you play inside an album works too. A video on its own, or a playlist someone made, doesn't name an album, and Tonaris says so.

## What doesn't work

- playlists, artist pages, podcasts and profiles;
- other services, such as Deezer, Bandcamp or Amazon Music;
- links with a part missing. Copy them again from the service.

When Tonaris can't read a link, it says why. You can still add the album by hand.

## How links open

Each album keeps at most one link for each service. Opening one is always your choice: choose a service's name on the album, or select the album and choose **Open in…**, or press <Keys k="O" />. Tonaris never opens a service for you.

- **Web page or app.** In **Settings**, then **Preferences**, **Open links in** chooses **Web page**, the setting at the start, or **App**. **App** opens Spotify, Apple Music, Qobuz and Tidal in their apps, on every device you use. A service whose app isn't installed won't open.
- **YouTube Music** has no desktop app, so its links always open its web page.
- **People who open your shared link** without signing in get the web pages.

More in [Listen in your streaming service](/help/browse/listen).
