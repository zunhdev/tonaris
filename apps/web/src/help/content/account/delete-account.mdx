Deleting your account removes it and everything you own in Tonaris, at once and for good. Nothing goes to Trash, and nobody can bring it back.

## Before you start

[Export your collection](/help/account/export) first, so you keep a copy. The dialog offers this too, as **Download your data first**. Tonaris can't restore your account from an export: it's a copy for you.

## Delete your account

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Account**.
2. At the bottom, under **Danger zone**, choose **Delete account**.
3. Read the list of what deleting means.
4. Type your sign-in email in the box under it.
5. Choose **Delete account permanently**.

**Cancel** closes the dialog and changes nothing.

## What happens at once

- You're signed out on every device, and your passkeys stop working.
- Your AI connections are disconnected.
- Everything you share becomes unavailable to everyone: the people you invited, and anyone with a link.
- Your email address no longer signs in.

Then Tonaris removes your data. The page says "Deleting your account…", then "Your account has been deleted". If it couldn't finish, it says so, with **Retry**.

## What's removed

- Your whole collection: folders, lists, albums, Unsorted and Trash, with your custom fields.
- Your settings and your profile.
- The shares saved in your **Shared with me**.

Your data leaves Tonaris at once, and leaves its encrypted backups within 30 days. If a backup is ever restored, your deletion is applied again before Tonaris opens.

<Note kind="careful">
  This can't be undone. Signing in later with the same email doesn't bring anything back or make a
  new account. To use Tonaris again, you need a new invitation.
</Note>

## What others shared with you

Folders and lists that other people shared with you are theirs, so they stay with them, unchanged. You lose your access to them, and you leave their **People**. Albums you added to someone else's folder or list are theirs too, so they stay.

<Note kind="phone">
  You can delete your account on a phone too, but downloading your data first needs a computer.
</Note>
