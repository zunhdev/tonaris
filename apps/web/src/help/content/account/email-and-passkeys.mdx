Both are in Settings, under **Account**: choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Account**. How signing in works is in [Joining and signing in](/help/start-here/joining-and-signing-in).

## Change your sign-in email

Tonaris checks both addresses, so nobody can move your account without your email.

1. Under **Sign-in email**, choose **Change sign-in email**.
2. Type the new address in **New email address**, and choose **Continue**.
3. Type the 6-digit code Tonaris sent to your current address, and choose **Continue**.
4. Type the code sent to the new address, and choose **Change email**.

The page says "Done — use" and your new address "to sign in".

- Each code works for 10 minutes. After 30 seconds, **Resend code** sends a new one.
- **Cancel** at any step changes nothing.
- If you leave the page halfway, the change waits for you at the step you reached.
- An address that's already on another Tonaris account can't be used.

After the change, your other devices are signed out. Sign in there with the new address: the old one no longer works. Your collection, sharing and passkeys stay as they are.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets your phone or computer sign you in with your face, fingerprint or screen lock. Email codes keep working too.

### Add a passkey

1. Under **Passkeys**, choose **Add a passkey**.
2. Follow what your device asks.

"Passkey added." shows, and Tonaris emails your sign-in address, naming the new passkey. If you get that email and didn't add a passkey, remove it here.

If you signed in more than a day ago, Tonaris first asks you to confirm it's you:

1. Type the code Tonaris sent to your sign-in email, and choose **Continue**.
2. Choose **Add a passkey** again.

A new passkey is named after the password manager that keeps it, such as "Google Password Manager", or "Passkey" when Tonaris can't tell. If this device already has one for your account, Tonaris says so and adds nothing. If your browser can't make passkeys, the page says "This browser can't add passkeys."

### Rename or remove a passkey

- Choose **Rename**, type a name that says where the passkey is, such as _Work laptop_, and choose **Save**. Or press <Keys k="Esc" /> to keep the old name.
- Choose **Remove** to remove it at once, without a question. It stops signing in right away. You can add it again later.

## Lost access to your email?

Changing your email here needs a code sent to your current address. If you can't get email there any more, the Tonaris creator can move your account to a new address: see [Lost access to your email?](/help/recovery). Moving it this way removes your passkeys, so add them again afterwards.
