Your profile is how the people you share with see you. To open it, choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Profile**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />, and choose **Profile**.

## Display name

This name shows instead of your email. Type it, then press <Keys k="Enter" /> or leave the field, and "Saved." shows. Your name also shows at the top of the sidebar, or your email if you have no name.

A display name is optional until you share. Inviting someone or turning on a link needs one, and while you share something, you can't clear it.

## Avatar color

Your avatar is the first letter of your name, in a color. Choose **Automatic**, which picks a color from your name, or one of 12 colors. Your choice saves at once, and your avatar shows in it everywhere.

## About

A line about you and your music, up to <Limit of="aboutLength" /> characters. It's saved like your name. To remove it, clear the field.

## Favorite albums

Pick up to <Limit of="favorites" /> of your albums to show the people you share with.

1. Choose **Add a favorite…**.
2. Type part of a title or artist, and choose the album.

Use the arrows on a favorite to move it up or down, and **×** to remove it. Each change saves at once.

If you delete a favorite album, it's marked "In Trash. It shows again if you restore it." Restore it, and it's back in its place. Delete it for good, and it leaves your favorites.

## Who sees what

Your people are the people you invited, unless they only declined, and the people who invited you, once you accepted.

| On your profile | Who sees it                                                                 |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Display name    | Your people, people you invite, and anyone who opens a link you share       |
| Avatar color    | The same people, beside your name                                           |
| About           | Your people: on your page in **People**, and when they choose **Shared by** |
| Favorite albums | Your people, on your page in **People**: covers, titles and artists only    |

Someone who opens a link you share sees your name and avatar, and nothing else of your profile.

Your About line and favorites never go into an [export](/help/account/export), and AI agents never see them. Exports leave out your display name too.
