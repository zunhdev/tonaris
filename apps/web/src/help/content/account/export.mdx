Your collection is yours to keep. An export is one ZIP file with everything you own in Tonaris: a complete file for programs and AI tools, and a copy you can read.

## Download your data

1. Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Data**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />, and choose **Data**.
2. Under **Download your data**, choose the **Download your data** button.

While Tonaris prepares it, the page says "Preparing your export…". You can go to other pages meanwhile: the download still starts. Then the page says the file's name, such as _tonaris-export-20261121-1430.zip_. **Download again** saves it once more, until your next export or until you sign out.

If the export fails, the page says why, with **Retry**.

## What's in the ZIP

- **tonaris-export.json**: everything, complete, for programs and AI tools.
- **tonaris-export.schema.json**: a description of that file's format, for programs.
- **collection.md**: a readable outline of your collection. Each folder is a heading and each list a table, in your order, with title, artist, year, type, links and your own fields. Unsorted and Trash follow, with Trash's dates.
- **README.md**: explains the files, in plain English.

The export holds:

- every album, including those in Unsorted and Trash. An album in several lists is in it once.
- your folders and lists, with their descriptions.
- your custom fields and their values.
- how you arranged and viewed each folder and list.

## What it leaves out

- Your sign-in email and your profile.
- Sharing: invitations, people and links.
- Shared with me, and anything other people own.
- AI connections.
- Cover pictures. Their web addresses are in the export, not the pictures.

<Note kind="info">
  Tonaris can't read an export back in. It's a copy for you to keep, or to use somewhere else.
</Note>

## How often you can export

Up to <Limit of="exportsPerHour" /> times an hour. After that, Tonaris says how many minutes to wait. An export that failed doesn't count.

## Your usage

Above the export, **Usage** shows how many albums you have out of <Limit of="albums" />, and how many of them are in Trash. Albums in Trash count until you [delete them for good](/help/trash/get-it-back). Restoring an album uses its old place again. See [Limits](/help/reference/limits).

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, Data shows your usage, but downloading your data needs a computer.
</Note>
