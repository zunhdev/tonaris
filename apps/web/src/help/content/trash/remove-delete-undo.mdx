Removing takes an album out of one list. Deleting moves a folder, list or album to Trash, where you can get it back for 30 days.

<Compare>
  <Choice title="Remove from a list">
    The album leaves one list. It stays in your collection and in its other lists, or goes to
    **Unsorted** if it was in no other list. **Undo** puts it back.
  </Choice>
  <Choice title="Delete from the collection">
    The album leaves every list and Unsorted, and goes to Trash. Tonaris asks first. You can restore
    it from Trash for 30 days.
  </Choice>
</Compare>

Removing and deleting are edits. Their buttons show in [Editing mode](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing), and their keys switch you to Editing mode by themselves.

## Take an album out of a list

1. Open the list and choose the album.
2. In the album panel, under **Lists**, choose **×** beside the list's name. Or press <Keys k="⌫" />.

A notice says **Removed from** and the list's name, with **Undo**, which puts the album back. In a list you arranged by hand, it goes back at the end.

To take several albums out at once, select them with <Keys k="X" />, then choose **Remove from** and the list's name in the bar under the albums.

In **Unsorted**, <Keys k="⌫" /> does nothing: an album there is in no list.

## Delete a list

1. Open the list, or find it in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Delete list** at the top of the list, or **Delete…** in the list's **⋯** menu in the sidebar. Or press <Keys k="⌫" />.

The list goes to Trash at once, and a notice says **1 list moved to Trash**, with **Undo**. Its albums stay in your other lists. An album that was in no other list goes to the top of **Unsorted**.

## Delete a folder

1. Choose **Delete folder** at the top of the folder, or **Delete…** in its **⋯** menu. Or press <Keys k="⌫" />.
2. Tonaris asks first, naming the folder and what's inside it. Its albums stay in their other lists or in Unsorted.
3. Choose the button that moves them, such as **Move 1 folder and 2 lists to Trash**.

There is no Undo for a folder. To get it back, [restore it from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back).

## Delete an album from your collection

1. Choose the album.
2. Choose **Delete from collection…**, the bin icon at the top of the album panel, beside its close button. Or press <Keys k="⌘ ⌫" />.
3. Tonaris names the lists the album leaves. Choose **Move to Trash**.

The bin icon stays at the top of the panel, so it's in view however long the album's details are.

To delete several albums, select them with <Keys k="X" /> and choose **Delete…** in the bar under the albums.

## What goes to Trash together

A folder goes to Trash with everything inside it, as one item. Restoring the folder brings all of it back.

Something you deleted on its own stays on its own. If you delete a list and later its folder, restoring the folder leaves the list in Trash. Albums you delete together by selecting them each go to Trash on their own.

## What can't be undone

Undo is a button in the notice, with no key. The notice stays 8 seconds, and longer while the pointer is on it.

<Note kind="careful">
  These are for good: deleting something permanently from Trash, emptying Trash, and anything left
  in Trash for 30 days. Tonaris then removes it, and nobody can bring it back.
</Note>

<Note kind="info">
  In a folder or list someone shares with you to edit, what you delete goes to its owner's Trash,
  and only they can restore it. See [Editing someone else's folder or
  list](/help/share/editing-a-share).
</Note>
