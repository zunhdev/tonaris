Everything you delete waits in Trash for 30 days. Until then you can restore it or delete it for good. After that, Tonaris deletes it for good by itself.

## Open Trash

Choose **Trash** in the sidebar, below your folders and lists. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="T" />.

Each row is something you deleted, newest first:

- **Contents** says what it is: an album, a list, or a folder with what was inside it.
- **Deleted** is the date you deleted it.
- **Expires** counts the days left, such as "Expires in 12 days". With less than 3 days left, it changes color. Point at it to see the exact date and time.
- **Was in** is where it was: **Collection** for the top level, or a folder's name.

To find something, type in **Filter by name** at the top.

Choose a folder's name to see what was inside it. Those folders and lists have no buttons of their own: they come back, or go, with the folder.

## Restore something

1. Open Trash and find the row.
2. Choose **Restore**. Or, with the focus on the row, press <Keys k="R" />.

A folder or list goes back into the folder it was in, with everything you deleted with it. An album goes back into its lists, or into **Unsorted** if none of them is left.

### When Tonaris asks where

If the folder it was in is in Trash too, or gone, Tonaris asks where to put it. Type in **Find a folder**, choose a folder or **Collection** for the top level, and choose **Restore**.

### When Tonaris asks for a name

If a folder or list with the same name is already there, type another name and choose **Restore**. To put it somewhere else, choose **Choose another location…**. Tonaris never renames anything by itself.

If you choose **Cancel**, it stays in Trash, and its days keep counting down.

### When others would see it

If what you restore would be inside something you share, Tonaris asks first and shows who will see it. Choose **Restore** or **Cancel**.

## What Restore skips

A restored list can't take back its albums that are in Trash, or deleted for good. A restored album can't go back into a list that's in Trash. Tonaris restores everything else, and the notice says how many it skipped. Choose **Details** to see each album and why.

## If the album is in your collection again

If you deleted an album and later added it again, restoring the old one would make two copies. Tonaris stops, shows both, and changes nothing until you choose:

- **Keep the active album** keeps the one in your collection. The old one stays in Trash.
- **Delete the active album, then restore…** moves the one in your collection to Trash, after asking, and restores the old one.

## Delete something for good

1. Choose **Delete permanently…** on its row.
2. Tonaris says what goes. Choose the button, such as **Permanently delete 1 item**.

To delete everything, choose **Empty Trash…** at the top.

<Note kind="careful">
  This can't be undone. A folder or list deleted for good doesn't take its albums: they stay in
  their other lists or in Unsorted. An album deleted for good goes with its links and field values.
</Note>

Albums in Trash count toward your <Limit of="albums" /> albums. Deleting one for good frees its place. See [Limits](/help/reference/limits).

<Note kind="info">
  When someone who edits a folder or list you share deletes something in it, it comes to your Trash.
</Note>

<Note kind="phone">
  Trash is on a computer only. On a phone, it says "Trash is on the desktop app."
</Note>
