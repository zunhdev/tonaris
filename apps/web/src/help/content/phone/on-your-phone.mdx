Tonaris works in your phone's browser: Safari on an iPhone, Chrome on Android. On a phone, it is for looking through your collection and listening. You make changes on a computer.

## What you can do on a phone

<Picture
  name="phone-collection"
  alt="Tonaris on a phone: the Collection screen with Mine selected, the folders Moods and Jazz, the lists Road trips, Discoveries 2026 and To listen, and Unsorted below them."
  caption="The first screen: your folders and lists, with Unsorted below them."
/>

- **Browse.** The first screen shows your folders and lists, with **Unsorted** below them. Tap a folder or list to open it, and tap the back arrow at the top left to go up. Two or more levels down, tap the name at the top to go straight to **Collection** or any folder above.
- **See what others share with you.** On the first screen, switch from **Mine** to **Shared**.
- **Search.** Tap the magnifying glass at the top to find folders and lists by name, and albums by title or artist.
- **Open an album.** Tap an album to see its details, then tap one of its links, such as **Apple Music**, to open it there.
- **Change how a view looks.** Tap the **View options** button at the top to choose the sort, the grouping, and rows or a grid.
- **Approve an agent's requests.** [Approvals](/help/agents/approvals) work fully on a phone.

<Note kind="tip">
  To open albums in your streaming apps rather than their websites, tap your account button at the
  top right, then **Settings**, then **Preferences**, and set **Open links in** to **App**.
</Note>

## What needs a computer

A phone is always in [Viewing mode](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing). These need a computer:

- adding albums, and making, renaming, moving or deleting folders and lists;
- changing an album's details, links or fields, and putting it in lists;
- rearranging by hand. A phone shows the order you set;
- sharing;
- **Trash**, setting up custom fields, and downloading your data.

Search on a phone offers commands that go somewhere, **View options** and **Open in…**. Other commands say **Edit on a computer**.

## Add Tonaris to your Home Screen

Install Tonaris on your phone, and it opens full screen from its own icon, like an app. It still needs an internet connection. [Install Tonaris as an app](/help/start-here/install-the-app) has the steps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Links and the installed app

A link to Tonaris, such as an agent's approval link, may open in your browser instead of the installed app. Both work. If Tonaris asks you to sign in there, sign in, and the page you wanted opens.

On a computer, when Tonaris is installed from Chrome or Edge, a Tonaris link goes to its window that is already open, rather than opening another.
