Each change here is made in the sharing dialog: open the folder or list, then choose **Share**, or press <Keys k="⇧ S" />. It saves at once, and the people it affects see the change straight away.

## Change what someone can do

1. In the person's row, open the menu that says **Can view** or **Can edit**.
2. Choose the other one.

The person doesn't need to accept again. Changed to **Can view**, they can't make any more changes, and a share they have open turns view-only.

A row may say _Also editable through "Jazz"_. That person can edit a folder above, so they can edit this one too, whatever its row says. To change that, change their role in the folder above.

## Remove someone's access

1. Choose **⋯** at the end of the person's row.
2. Choose **Remove access**.

It works on pending and accepted invitations, and it doesn't ask first. The person can no longer open the folder or list, and in their Shared with me it shows as "No longer available". If they also have the link, the link still works for them: turn it off too. To share with them again, invite them again.

## Turn the link off

1. Turn off **Link sharing**.
2. Tonaris asks first. Choose **Turn off**.

The link stops working for good, for everyone who has it. Turning link sharing on again makes a new, different link: the old one never works again. People who kept the old link in their Shared with me see it as "No longer available" until they open the new link and add it again.

## Stop sharing with one person everywhere

To end everything you share with someone in one step, open their page in **People** and choose **Stop sharing with** and their name, such as **Stop sharing with Sam…**. [People](/help/share/people) explains how.

## What stopping doesn't change

- What people who could edit added or changed stays in your collection.
- What they share with you stays shared.

<Note kind="careful">
  Stopping can't take back copies. Anyone who could open a folder or list may have copied it into
  their own collection, and that copy stays theirs.
</Note>
