When someone invites you with **Can edit**, you help build their folder or list. What you add or change is saved in their collection, and they see it at once, as does everyone they share it with. Their changes reach you the same way.

## How you know you can edit

- The invitation, and its email, say "to edit".
- In Shared with me it says "you can edit", and the sidebar shows a pencil after its name.
- At the top of the folder or list: "Can edit · Shared by" and the owner's name.

If you can edit a folder, you can edit everything inside it.

## Start editing

1. Open the folder or list from **Shared with me** in the sidebar.
2. Switch to **Edit**, or press <Keys k="E" />.

The sidebar keeps showing your own collection. Your sort, grouping and layout of the share are your own.

## What you can do

Inside the share you can:

- Make lists and folders: choose **New list** or **New folder** at the top of a folder, or press <Keys k="N" /> or <Keys k="⇧ N" />.
- Rename folders and lists: choose **Rename** at the top, or press <Keys k="F2" />. You can also give them descriptions, colors and icons.
- Move them to other folders in the share: press <Keys k="M" /> for **Move to…**.
- Add albums to its lists, from links or by hand: choose **Add album** in the sidebar, or press <Keys k="C" />, and pick one of the share's lists.
- Change an album's details, cover, links and custom-field values. You see all the owner's fields. The change shows wherever the album is in their collection.
- Take albums out of its lists, and delete folders, lists and albums.

## What stays with the owner

- **The shared folder or list itself.** You can rename it, describe it, and set its color and icon, but not move or delete it.
- **Where things go.** Nothing moves out of the share, to the top of their collection, or into another share. **Move to…** offers only the share's folders.
- **Custom fields.** You fill them in, but can't add, rename or delete fields or their choices.
- **Sharing.** You can't invite people or turn on a link, and you don't see who else has access.
- **Their Trash and their settings.**

## Deleting

Deleting an album (press <Keys k="⌘ ⌫" />) takes it out of every list in the share. Tonaris asks first, such as "Delete 'Field Notes' from 'Jazz'?". If the owner has the album in other lists, it stays there. If not, it goes to their Trash.

Deleting a list (press <Keys k="⌫" />) moves it to the owner's Trash, with **Undo** right after. A folder asks first. Once it's in their Trash, only the owner can restore it.

## When you can no longer edit

If the owner changes you to **Can view**, the share turns view-only. If they remove your access, it says it's no longer available. Changes not saved by then are reported, such as "2 changes weren't saved". What you added before stays in the owner's collection.

## AI agents

An AI agent you connected with **Manage collection** can change the share as you can. One connected as **Read-only** can only read it. Accepting invitations and leaving shares happen in Tonaris itself.

<Note kind="phone">On a phone, a share you can edit opens view-only. Edit it on a computer.</Note>
