Sharing happens in the sharing dialog. There you invite people, choose what each one can do, and turn the link on or off.

## Open the sharing dialog

1. Open the folder or list you want to share.
2. Choose **Share** at the top of the page, or press <Keys k="⇧ S" />.

From the sidebar: choose **⋯** beside a folder or list, or press <Keys k="⇧ F10" /> on it, then choose **Share…**.

The dialog starts by saying what sharing means: a folder includes everything inside it, now and in the future, and people who can edit can add, change, move and delete what's inside. Read it before you share.

## Add a display name first

People you share with see your display name, not your email. If you don't have one yet, the dialog asks for it under **Add a display name to share**. Type a name and choose **Save**. While you share anything, you can change your name but not remove it.

## Invite people

1. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit** in the menu beside **Invite by name or email…**.
2. Type the person's email address.
3. Choose **Invite**, or press <Keys k="Enter" />.

When you select the field, your [people](/help/share/people) show under it, the most recent first. Type part of a name to find someone else. Choose a person, or move to them with <Keys k="↑" /> and <Keys k="↓" /> and press <Keys k="Enter" />, to invite them at once with the role beside the field.

The person needs a Tonaris account with that email. If no account uses it, the dialog says so and keeps what you typed, so you can fix a typo or share a link instead.

## Who has access

Each person you invite gets a row: their name, their email, then **Pending** until they accept, and **Accepted** after. A pending row also says how its email is doing: **sending…**, **sent**, **email failed** or **email not confirmed**. For the last two, choose **Resend**. The invitation is saved either way, and the person also finds it in Tonaris.

The email says who invited them, to which folder or list, and whether to view or to edit. Its link works only for them: forwarding it gives no one access.

When a folder above is shared too, the dialog says so, for example _Also visible through "Jazz": 1 person_. Choose the folder's name to open its sharing dialog.

## Share a link

1. Turn on **Link sharing**.
2. Choose **Copy**. The button then says **Copied**.
3. Paste the link where you like: a message, an email, a post.

Anyone with the link can view the folder or list and send the link on. It's the same link each time you open the dialog. A link only lets people view: to let someone edit, invite them.

## See what you share

In the sidebar and on folder pages, a shared folder or list has a small mark after its name: people for invitations, a chain link for a link, or both. A paler mark means it's shared through a folder above.

<Note kind="info">
  Sharing needs a connection. Offline, the dialog says "You're offline. Sharing waits until you're
  back."
</Note>

<Note kind="phone">**Share** isn't on a phone. Share from a computer.</Note>
