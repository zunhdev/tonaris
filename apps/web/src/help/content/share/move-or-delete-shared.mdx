Sharing a folder shares everything inside it. So where a folder or list sits decides who can see it.

## Moving into or out of a shared folder

- Move a folder or list **into** a shared folder, and the people and the link of that folder see it too.
- Move it **out**, and the people who saw it only through that folder no longer see it.
- Its own sharing goes with it. If you shared the list itself, by invitation or by link, that still works after the move.

## Tonaris shows you first

When you move a folder or list with **Move to…** (press <Keys k="M" />) or by dragging it onto a folder in the sidebar, Tonaris shows what the move would change:

- **Newly visible to** … : who would start seeing it.
- **No longer visible through** a folder: who would stop seeing it, and through which folder they saw it.
- **Still shared directly with** … : its own sharing, which stays.
- **Sharing unchanged**: nobody gains or loses it.

People who haven't accepted yet show with "(invited)". If someone can still see it another way, Tonaris doesn't say they lose it.

When a move changes who can see it, Tonaris asks before moving, such as "Move 'To listen' to 'Jazz'?". Choose **Move** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to leave it where it is. A move that changes nothing happens at once.

## Deleting something you share

While a folder or list is in Trash, nobody it's shared with can open it, by invitation or by link. Its invitations and its link are kept, not ended.

When you delete a folder, with **Delete…** in its **⋯** menu in the sidebar or by pressing <Keys k="⌫" />, Tonaris asks first. When the folder, something in it, or a folder above it is shared, the question says that its people and links can't open it while it's in Trash, and that restoring it gives them access again. A list goes to Trash at once, with **Undo**, and its sharing pauses the same way.

Meanwhile, people see "No longer available" in Tonaris. A link says "This link isn't available. Ask the person who shared it for a new one."

After 30 days in Trash, it's deleted for good, and its sharing ends.

## Restoring it

Restoring it in Trash, with **Restore** or by pressing <Keys k="R" />, turns its sharing back on: accepted invitations, and the link if you didn't turn it off, work again. Invitations nobody answered yet are still waiting. Access you removed, and a link you turned off, while it was in Trash stay that way.

When restoring would let anyone see it, Tonaris asks first, such as "Restore 'Late night'?" with "Once it's back, others can see it." and who. Choose **Restore**. **Undo** right after a deletion doesn't ask. [Get something back from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back) explains restoring.
