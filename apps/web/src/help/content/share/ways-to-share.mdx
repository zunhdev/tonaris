You share one folder or one list at a time, never your whole collection. You can use several ways on the same folder or list: some people can view it, others can edit it, and its link can be on too.

## The three ways

|                   | Invite to view                                                                                 | Invite to edit                                              | Share a link                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who can use it    | The person you invite                                                                          | The person you invite                                       | Anyone who has the link                                                                       |
| Account needed    | Yes, a Tonaris account                                                                         | Yes, a Tonaris account                                      | No                                                                                            |
| What they can do  | Look through it, open its albums in their streaming service, copy it into their own collection | All of that, and add, change, move and delete what's inside | Look through it and open its albums. Signed in, they can also keep it in their Shared with me |
| Always up to date | Yes                                                                                            | Yes                                                         | Yes                                                                                           |
| Can you stop it   | Yes, at any time                                                                               | Yes, or change it to view                                   | Yes: turning the link off ends it for good                                                    |

An invited person sees nothing until they accept. A link works as soon as you turn it on, and anyone who has it can send it on.

Tonaris is invitation-only for now, and inviting someone to a folder or list doesn't give them an account. For a friend without one, share a link.

"Always up to date" means people see what's there now. When you add an album, it shows for them within a few seconds, without reloading.

## What sharing a folder includes

Everything inside it: its folders, its lists and their albums. What comes later is included too. A list you make inside the folder, or move into it, is shared at once. Move a list out, and the people who saw it only through the folder no longer see it. [Moving or deleting what you share](/help/share/move-or-delete-shared) explains more.

## What people see

- The names, descriptions, colors and icons of the shared folders and lists.
- The albums in its lists, with title, artist, year, cover and streaming links.
- Custom fields: people who can edit see all your fields, so they can fill them in. People who can only view don't see custom fields.
- Your display name and avatar. Never your email address.

Each person sorts and lays out what you share their own way. Your sort and your order stay yours.

## What stays private

- Every folder and list outside the share, and the folders above it, even their names.
- Albums that are only in your other lists, and the albums in Unsorted.
- Which of your other lists an album is in.
- Your Trash and your settings.

<Note kind="info">
  Only you can share your folders and lists, in Tonaris on a computer. People who can edit can't
  share them further, and AI agents can't invite anyone or turn a link on.
</Note>
