Someone can share a folder or list with you by inviting you, or by sending you its link.

## Answer an invitation

You hear about an invitation in an email, such as "Robin invited you to view "Late night" on Tonaris", and in Tonaris: a notice if it's open, and **Invitations** in the sidebar while any wait for you.

1. Choose **Invitations** in the sidebar, or **View** in the notice, or open the link in the email.
2. Choose **Accept** or **Decline**.

Each invitation says who it's from, the folder or list, the date, and "to edit" when you're invited to edit. Nothing is shared with you until you accept. Accepting puts it in Shared with me.

The email's link works only when you're signed in with the address it was sent to. An invitation that was withdrawn, or that you declined, says "This invitation is no longer available."

## Shared with me

**Shared with me** in the sidebar lists what's shared with you, each with its owner's name, such as _Summer 2026 · Mira_. A pencil after it means you can edit it. Choose **Shared with me** itself to see them all, by owner, each marked "by invitation" or "from a link".

A shared folder or list opens with "View only · Shared by Mira" at the top, or "Can edit" when you can edit it. Choose it to see more about Mira. What you see is always up to date: when Mira adds an album, it shows without reloading.

How it looks is up to you. Choose **View**, or press <Keys k="V" />, to sort, group, or show covers. Your choices are saved for you on all your devices, and never change Mira's.

## Copy one into your collection

On a computer, choose **Copy to my collection…** at the top of a shared folder or list. Choose where the copy goes, check its name, and choose **Copy**. The copy is yours: what's inside now, with no link to the original. Albums you already have join its lists instead of being copied twice. If the copy would go over a limit, nothing is copied, and Tonaris says which limit.

## Open a shared link

A link opens in any browser, without an account. You see the folder or list, who shared it, and the albums with their streaming links. You can sort it and switch between rows and a grid, for this visit only.

When you're signed in to Tonaris, the page offers **Add to Shared with me**. Choose it to keep the share; it then says **In Shared with me**. Opening a link on its own adds nothing.

A link that says "This link isn't available. Ask the person who shared it for a new one." was turned off, or what it shares is in Trash.

## Leave or remove a share

On the Shared with me page:

- For one from an invitation, choose **Leave share…**, then **Leave**. To come back, you'd need a new invitation.
- For one from a link, choose **Remove from Shared with me**.

Nothing changes for the owner or anyone else.

## No longer available

When the owner stops sharing with you, turns off the link you kept, or moves it to Trash, it shows dimmed as "No longer available". Choose **Remove** to take it off your list. One in Trash comes back if the owner restores it.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, switch the Collection screen to **Shared**: your invitations, People and what's shared
  with you are there. Copying needs a computer.
</Note>
