Tonaris has no friend requests, and no way to look up other members. Your people come only from invitations:

- everyone you invited to a folder or list, unless they only ever declined;
- everyone who invited you, once you accepted.

Someone stays one of your people after a share ends, whoever ended it. Links don't add anyone.

## The People page

Choose **People** in the sidebar. Each person shows their avatar, their name, the email you typed for them, and what's shared each way, such as "You share 1 folder (1 to edit)" and "Shares 1 list with you". The people you shared with most recently come first. To find someone, type part of their name or email in **Find people…**.

## A person's page

Choose a person to see:

- their About text and favorite albums, if they added them (see below);
- **You share with** them: each folder or list, whether they **Can view** or **Can edit**, and **Pending** or **Accepted**. On a computer, choosing one opens its sharing dialog;
- what they **share with you**: choosing one opens it, or its invitation while it waits for your answer.

## Your profile

Your people see your About text and up to <Limit of="favorites" /> favorite albums on your page in their People. They also see your About in the card that opens from "Shared by" on what you share. Anyone else, including people with a link, sees only your name and avatar. You add these in Settings: [Your profile and who sees it](/help/account/profile).

## Suggestions when you invite

In the sharing dialog, your people show under the invite field, the 8 most recent first. Type part of a name or email to find the others. People already invited to that folder or list aren't shown.

Someone who invited you shows by name only: you see their email only if you typed it yourself. When two people have the same name, each says what they share with you, such as _Mira · shares "Summer 2026" with you_.

## Stop sharing with someone

1. On the person's page, such as Sam's, choose **Stop sharing with Sam…**.
2. Check the list of what Sam will lose, then choose **Stop sharing with Sam**.

They lose everything you share with them at once, including what's in your Trash. What they share with you stays, and they stay in People. This can't be undone: to share again, invite them again.

## Remove someone from People

On their page, choose **Remove from People**. They no longer show on the People page or in the invite field, and they no longer see your About or favorites. Nothing you share with them stops. They come back when one of you invites the other again.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, find **People** on the Collection screen's **Shared** side. You can look at it there;
  stopping sharing and removing people need a computer.
</Note>
