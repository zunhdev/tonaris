You change fields in **Settings**, then **Custom fields**. Press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" /> to open Settings. The table shows each field's type, its options and how many albums use it, such as "Used by 12 albums · 2 in Trash". Choose a field's name to open it.

## Rename a field

1. Choose the field's name to open it.
2. Change its **Name** and press <Keys k="Enter" />. <Keys k="Esc" /> puts back the old name.

The new name shows on every album at once, in Trash too, and every value stays as it was. Like a new field, it can't take the name of another of your fields.

## Change its type

You can change a field's **Type** only while no album uses it: not an album in Trash, and not an album with the field added but empty. When an album does, the **Type** is locked, with a line such as "Used by 3 albums. Create a new field of the type you need instead." and a **Create new field** button.

Why: a new type would mean turning every value into something else, such as dates into numbers. Tonaris never changes or throws away your values to make that possible. So make a new field of the type you need, fill it in, then delete the old one.

While no album uses it:

- Between **Single choice** and **Multiple choice**, the options stay as they are.
- From a choice type to any other type, the options go. Tonaris lists them first and changes the type only when you choose **Change type**. **Keep options** leaves it as it was. Changing back later doesn't bring the options back.

## Change the options

- **Rename an option:** change its text and press <Keys k="Enter" />. Every album that chose it shows the new name.
- **Add an option:** type it in the **New option** box and press <Keys k="Enter" />, or choose **Add option**. It goes at the end.
- **Remove an option:** choose **×** next to it. If no album chose it, it goes at once.

If any album chose the option, Tonaris asks first, in a message such as "Remove option 'Cassette'?" with how many albums chose it. Confirm with **Remove from 3 albums**, or whatever number it shows. Removing it clears it from those albums, in Trash too. A single choice becomes empty, and a multiple choice keeps its other options. The field stays on those albums.

<Note kind="careful">
  Removing an option that albums chose can't be undone. Adding an option with the same name later
  makes a new one that no album has chosen.
</Note>

## Delete a field

1. Choose the field's name to open it.
2. Choose **Delete field**.
3. Tonaris shows what goes: the field, its options, and how many albums have it. Choose **Delete field** again to confirm.

Tonaris always asks, even when no album uses the field.

<Note kind="careful">
  Deleting a field takes it and its values off every album, in Trash too. It can't be undone: the
  field doesn't go to Trash, and getting an album back from Trash doesn't bring the field back.
</Note>

You can then make a new field with the same name. It starts on no album.

## Albums you share

When some of the albums are in a folder or list you share, the confirmation says so. For example: "2 of these albums are in what you share: the people you share them with stop seeing 'Cassette' on them."

People who can edit your folder or list fill in values, but only you can rename, change, add, remove or delete fields and options.
