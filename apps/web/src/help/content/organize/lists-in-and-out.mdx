You can put an album in a list from the album, by dragging it onto the list, or together with other albums. Taking an album out of a list keeps it in your collection: in its other lists, or in Unsorted.

These are changes, so Tonaris switches to [Editing mode](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing) when you start one.

## Add an album to a list

1. Select the album in a list or in Unsorted, or open it in the album panel.
2. Press <Keys k="L" />. Or, in the album panel in Editing mode, choose **Add to list** under **Lists**.
3. Type part of the list's name, or of a folder it's in: _moods night_ finds _Moods › Late night_.
4. Choose the list. Or move to it with <Keys k="↓" /> and <Keys k="↑" />, and press <Keys k="Enter" />.

Before you type, the dialog shows the lists you added albums to last, under **Recent**. It offers only the lists the album isn't in yet.

## Drag an album onto a list

In Editing mode, with the albums shown as rows:

1. Point at the album's row. A handle shows at its left edge.
2. Drag the handle onto a list in the sidebar, and let go.

The album is now in that list too, and stays in the one you're in. To **move** it instead, hold <Keys k="⌥" /> as you let go: it leaves the list you're in and joins the other, with **Undo**.

As you drag, a list that can take the album lights up. Folders, and lists the album is already in, don't. Albums shown as covers have no handle: use <Keys k="L" /> there.

## Work with several albums

In Editing mode, in a list or Unsorted:

1. Press <Keys k="X" /> on an album to select it. Or point at it and choose its checkbox.
2. Select more the same way. <Keys k="⇧ ↑" /> and <Keys k="⇧ ↓" /> also select the album above or below.
3. In the bar under the albums, choose **Add to list…**, **Remove from** the list on view, or **Delete…**. Or press <Keys k="L" />, <Keys k="⌫" /> or <Keys k="⌘ ⌫" />.

The bar says how many albums are selected. To clear the selection, press <Keys k="Esc" /> or choose **×** in the bar.

## Take an album out of a list

- In the list, select the album and press <Keys k="⌫" />.
- Or, in the album panel in Editing mode, choose **×** next to the list's name under **Lists**.

A message says "Removed from Late night", with **Undo**. The album stays in its other lists, or goes to [Unsorted](/help/organize/one-album-many-lists#unsorted). In Unsorted, <Keys k="⌫" /> does nothing to an album.

<Note kind="info">
  <Keys k="⌫" /> with a folder or list selected in the sidebar deletes that folder or list. Select
  the album first.
</Note>

## Remove or delete?

<Compare>
  <Choice title="Remove from a list">
    The album leaves this one list. It stays in your collection, in its other lists or in Unsorted.
    **Undo** puts it back.
  </Choice>
  <Choice title="Delete from your collection">
    The album leaves every list and Unsorted, and goes to Trash for 30 days. Tonaris asks first.

    panel, **Delete from collection…**.
  </Choice>
