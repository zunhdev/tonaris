On a computer, Tonaris has two modes. **Viewing** shows your albums, folders and lists with nothing in the way. **Editing** adds the buttons, boxes and handles you need to change them. On a computer you haven't used Tonaris on before, it starts in Viewing mode.

## Why two modes

Most of the time you look through your albums and listen. In Viewing mode, the screen stays calm, and a click can't rename or move something by mistake. When you want to sort things out, switch to Editing.

## Switch modes

At the top of a folder, list or Unsorted, choose **View** or **Edit**. Or press <Keys k="E" />.

In Editing mode, the line under the top of the page takes the accent color, so you can always tell which mode you're in. The mode is the same in every view, and stays as you left it on that computer, even after you close it. Each computer keeps its own.

## What Editing mode adds

- Checkboxes, to [select several albums](/help/organize/lists-in-and-out#work-with-several-albums) at once.
- Dragging, by a row's handle or by a card: albums onto lists, and anything into your own order under **Manual**.
- Album titles, artists and years you change in place: double-click one, or press <Keys k="Enter" /> on it.
- In the album panel: fields you can change, **Add to list**, **Add field**, and **Delete from collection…**.
- At the top of a folder or list: **Rename**, **Description**, and **Delete folder** or **Delete list**.

Some things work the same in both modes: opening folders, lists and albums, the **View** options, and rearranging with <Keys k="⌥ ↑" /> and <Keys k="⌥ ↓" />.

## Starting a change switches for you

You don't have to switch first. When you start a change in Viewing mode, Tonaris switches to Editing and says "Editing mode on". It does this when you, for example:

- make a folder or list (<Keys k="N" />, <Keys k="⇧ N" />), rename one (<Keys k="F2" />) or move one (<Keys k="M" />)
- add albums (<Keys k="C" />) or paste a link to add one
- put an album in a list (<Keys k="L" />), or take it out (<Keys k="⌫" />)
- select an album (<Keys k="X" />)
- give a folder or list a color or an icon

## Leaving Editing mode never loses a change

If you're in the middle of a change, such as typing a new name, and switch to View, Tonaris saves it first. If what you typed can't be saved, such as an album title left empty or a name that's taken, Tonaris stays in Editing mode and shows why. Fix it, or press <Keys k="Esc" /> to drop it.

## A mode doesn't change what you can do

The mode only changes what the screen shows. It sends nothing to Tonaris, and it never gives anyone more rights.

A folder or list someone shares with you to view shows **View only** at the top, with no **View** and **Edit** switch. One shared with you to edit shows **Can edit** and the switch. [Editing someone else's folder or list](/help/share/editing-a-share) tells you more.

<Note kind="phone">
  On a phone, Tonaris is for looking through your collection, searching it and opening albums.
  There's no **View** and **Edit** switch, and an album's details show without anything to change.
  The same goes for a very narrow window on a computer.
</Note>
