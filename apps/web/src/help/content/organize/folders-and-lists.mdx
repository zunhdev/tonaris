A list is a set of albums: _To listen_, _Late night_, _Best of 1974_. A folder holds lists and other folders: a folder _Moods_ can hold the lists _Late night_ and _Rainy Sundays_. You see them all in the sidebar, under **Collection**.

## What each one holds

- **A list** holds albums. It can't hold folders or other lists.
- **A folder** holds lists and folders, but no albums of its own. To put albums in a folder, put them in a list inside it.

Folders can hold folders, up to <Limit of="depth" /> levels deep. One album can be in many lists at once: see [One album, many lists, and Unsorted](/help/organize/one-album-many-lists).

## Make a folder or list

1. In the sidebar, select a folder or list, or open one.
2. Press <Keys k="N" /> for a new list, or <Keys k="⇧ N" /> for a new folder.
3. Type a name and press <Keys k="Enter" />. To cancel, press <Keys k="Esc" />.

The new one goes inside the folder you selected or opened, or next to the list you selected or opened. With nothing selected or open, it goes at the top level.

Other ways to make one:

- **At the top level:** choose **+** beside **Collection** in the sidebar, then **New list** or **New folder**.
- **From a folder's or list's menu:** point at it in the sidebar and choose its **⋯** button, or select it and press <Keys k="⇧ F10" />. Then choose **New list** or **New folder**. On a folder, the new one goes inside it; on a list, next to it.

Making a folder or list switches Tonaris to [Editing mode](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing) for you.

## Rename a folder or list

1. In the sidebar, select the folder or list.
2. Press <Keys k="F2" />. Or choose its **⋯** button, then **Rename**.
3. Type the new name and press <Keys k="Enter" />.

In Editing mode, an open folder or list also has **Rename** at the top of its page. A new name shows everywhere at once, and everything inside stays as it was.

## Names

- **No two the same in one place.** A folder or list can't have the same name as another folder or list next to it, even with different capital letters: a folder _Jazz_ and a list _jazz_ can't sit side by side. The same name in two different folders is fine.
- **Tonaris tells you before saving.** A taken name keeps the name box open with a message such as "'Late night' already exists in Moods. Choose another name."
- **Length:** a name can have up to <Limit of="nameLength" /> characters.

## How many you can have

You can have <Limit of="containers" /> folders and lists in all, counting the ones in Trash until they're gone for good. At the limit, you can't make a new one, but all the ones you have keep working.

## A folder's page

Open a folder and it shows what it holds as cards. A list's card shows its first three album covers, its name and how many albums it holds. A folder's card shows covers from the lists inside it.

To see rows instead, choose **View**, or press <Keys k="V" />, then **Rows** under **Layout**. Rows also show the first line of each description. Tonaris remembers the choice for that folder. [Covers or rows, sorting and grouping](/help/browse/covers-or-rows) tells you more.
