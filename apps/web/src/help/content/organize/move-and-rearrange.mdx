Moving puts a folder or list in another folder. Rearranging changes the order of what a view shows, such as ranking the albums in a list. Neither one makes a copy.

## Move a folder or list

1. Select the folder or list in the sidebar, or open it.
2. Press <Keys k="M" />. Or choose its **⋯** button in the sidebar, then **Move to…**.
3. Type part of the folder's name, such as _jazz_.
4. Choose the folder, or **Collection** for the top level. Or move to it with <Keys k="↓" /> and press <Keys k="Enter" />.

Before you type, **Collection** comes first, then the folders you moved things into last, under **Recent**. The dialog offers only places the folder or list can go, so not the folder it's in now.

A folder moves with everything in it. A list keeps its albums, description, color and icon. If the new place already has a folder or list with the same name, Tonaris says so, and nothing moves.

You can also drag a folder or list onto a folder in the sidebar, or onto **Collection**.

## Move albums from one list to another

In Editing mode, drag the album by its handle onto the other list in the sidebar, and hold <Keys k="⌥" /> as you let go. It leaves the list you're in and joins the other, with **Undo**. Without <Keys k="⌥" />, it joins the other list and stays in this one too. [Put albums in lists and take them out](/help/organize/lists-in-and-out#drag-an-album-onto-a-list) has the details.

## Put things in your own order

Your own order is the **Manual** sort. Each list, each folder, the top level and Unsorted keep their own.

1. Choose **View**, or press <Keys k="V" />, and set **Sort** to **Manual**.
2. Select an album, folder or list in the view.
3. Press <Keys k="⌥ ↑" /> or <Keys k="⌥ ↓" /> to move it up or down by one place.
4. Press <Keys k="⌥ ⇧ ↑" /> or <Keys k="⌥ ⇧ ↓" /> to move it to the top or the bottom.

**With the mouse:** in Editing mode, point at a row, then drag the handle at its left edge to where you want it.

**To a numbered place:** select the item, press <Keys k="⌘ K" />, choose **Move to position…**, type the number and choose **Move**.

Under another sort, nothing moves, and Tonaris offers **Switch to manual order**.

Good to know:

- An album you add to a list goes at its end. An album arriving in Unsorted goes at its top.
- The sidebar shows the folders and lists in the order their folder's page shows them.
- Your order is yours alone. People you share a list with keep their own order of it.

## Moving something you share

Moving can change who sees a folder or list. Move a list into a folder you share, and the people you share that folder with can see the list too. Move it out, and they stop seeing it.

**Move to…** shows this for the folder you're on before you choose. When who sees it would change, Tonaris asks first, in a message such as "Move 'Road trips' to 'Jazz'?", listing who gains or loses it. [Moving or deleting what you share](/help/share/move-or-delete-shared) tells you more.
