Colors and icons help you find a folder or list in the sidebar. A description tells you, and the people you share with, what it's for.

## Give it a color

1. In the sidebar, point at the folder or list and choose its **⋯** button. Or select it and press <Keys k="⇧ F10" />.
2. Choose **Color**, then one of the eight colors, or **None**.

Or select it, press <Keys k="⌘ K" /> and choose **Color…**.

**A folder's color passes to what's inside.** A folder or list without a color of its own takes the color of the nearest folder above it. Give it its own color, and it keeps that one. So when the folder _Moods_ is amber, its lists are amber too, except one you color teal.

The color shows in the sidebar, on folder pages, and on the list names in the album panel.

## Give it an icon

1. Choose the folder's or list's **⋯** button in the sidebar, then **Icon…**. Or select it, press <Keys k="⌘ K" /> and choose **Icon…**.
2. Choose an icon, such as **Guitar**, **Saxophone** or **Moon**. **None** brings back the usual folder or list symbol.

The icon takes the place of the folder or list symbol in the sidebar, on folder pages and in search, drawn in its color.

**An icon stays on its own folder or list.** The lists inside a folder with an icon keep their usual list symbol, in the folder's color.

## Write a description

1. Open the folder or list, and switch to [Editing mode](/help/organize/viewing-and-editing) with <Keys k="E" />.
2. At the top, choose the **Description** button, next to **Rename**. Or click the description itself.
3. Type in the panel that opens on the right.
4. Choose **Done**, or press <Keys k="⌘ Enter" />.

Pressing <Keys k="Esc" />, closing the panel or leaving Editing mode saves it too. To remove a description, delete all its text.

### What it can hold

A description takes simple formatting, called Markdown:

- `**bold**` and `*italic*`, or the **Bold** (<Keys k="⌘ B" />) and **Italic** (<Keys k="⌘ I" />) buttons
- lists: lines that start with `- `, or with `1. `
- links to web pages, written as `[text](https://…)` or as a plain address. They open in a new tab.

Everything else shows as you wrote it, line breaks included. A description can have up to <Limit of="descriptionLength" /> characters, and the panel counts them as you type.

### How it shows

Under the folder's or list's name, the first three lines show, and **More** shows the rest. On a folder's page in rows, its first line follows its name. People you share it with see it the same way, and so does anyone who opens its link.

Search doesn't look in descriptions. To find a folder or list, search for its name.
