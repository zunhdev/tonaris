An album is in your collection once. Putting it in a list doesn't make a copy: every list it's in shows the same album. When it's in no list at all, it waits in **Unsorted**.

## One album in many lists

Say _Blue Hour Sessions_ is in _Late night_ and in _Hard bop_. Both lists show the one album, so:

- **A change shows everywhere.** Fix its year while you're in _Late night_, and _Hard bop_ shows the new year too. The same goes for its cover, its links and [your own fields](/help/fields/custom-fields).
- **It's in each list once.** **Add to list…** offers only the lists it isn't in yet.
- **Your collection counts it once,** however many lists it's in.

To see which lists an album is in, open it. The album panel shows them under **Lists**, and each name opens its list.

## Unsorted

**Unsorted**, in the sidebar, holds every album that's in no list. Tonaris keeps it up to date for you:

- An album you add without choosing a list goes to Unsorted.
- An album you take out of its last list goes to Unsorted.
- An album you put in a list leaves Unsorted.

Albums in Unsorted keep all their details, count in your collection, and show up in search. Think of Unsorted as an inbox: now and then, go through it and put its albums in lists. Sorted by **Manual**, it shows the albums that arrived last at the top.

To go there, choose **Unsorted** in the sidebar, or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="U" />.

## Deleting a list keeps its albums

When a list goes to Trash, its albums stay in your collection. Each one stays in its other lists, and one that was in no other list goes to Unsorted. Deleting a folder works the same way for every list inside it.

[Get the list back from Trash](/help/trash/get-it-back) within 30 days, and its albums are in it again.

To take an album out of your collection, you delete the album itself. [Remove, delete and undo](/help/trash/remove-delete-undo) explains the difference.
