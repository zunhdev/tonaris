## Joining Tonaris

Tonaris is invitation-only for now. When you're invited, an email arrives with a link to join.

1. Open the link in the email. The page shows the address the invitation is for.
2. Choose **Continue**. Tonaris emails that address a 6-digit code.
3. Type the code, or open the link in that email.
4. Choose a display name, the name the people you share with will see. You can skip it and choose one later.

Your collection opens, empty and ready for [your first album](/help/start-here/your-first-ten-minutes). Joining means you agree to the [terms of use](/terms) and the [privacy notice](/privacy).

## Signing in

  name="sign-in"

  caption="Sign in asks for your email, then for the code Tonaris sends to it."
/>

1. Go to [tonaris.app/sign-in](/sign-in), type your email, and choose **Continue**.
2. Type the 6-digit code from the email, or open the link in it.

A code works for 10 minutes. If the email hasn't arrived, look in your spam folder. After 30 seconds you can ask for a new code.

Opened the link on another phone or computer? Tonaris asks there whether to sign in on that device, so a link opened by mistake signs nobody in.

You stay signed in on each device for 30 days after you last used Tonaris there.

## Signing in with a passkey

A passkey lets your phone or computer sign you in with your face, fingerprint or screen lock, without an email.

1. Sign in with a code first. Then choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Account**.
2. Choose **Add a passkey** and follow what your device asks.

Next time, choose **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page. Your browser may also offer the passkey when you select the email field.

## Signing out

Choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Sign out**. If some of your changes haven't been saved yet, Tonaris asks before it signs you out, so nothing is lost by accident.

## Lost access to your email?

If you can't get email at your sign-in address any more, the Tonaris creator can move your account to a new address. Your collection, sharing and settings stay as they are. [Here's what to do](/help/recovery).
