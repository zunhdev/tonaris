Do this on a computer: adding albums and making lists need one. You'll need a link to an album from Spotify, Apple Music, Qobuz, Tidal or YouTube Music.

## 1. Make a list

1. In the sidebar, choose **+** beside **Collection**, then **New list**. Or press <Keys k="N" />.
2. Type a name, such as _Late night_, and press <Keys k="Enter" />.

Your new list shows in the sidebar. Choose it to open it: it's empty for now.

## 2. Copy an album's link

In your streaming app or on its website, find an album you like. Open its share menu and copy the album's link. A link to one of its songs works too: Tonaris finds the album the song is on.

## 3. Add the album

1. In Tonaris, with your new list open, choose **Add album** in the sidebar. Or press <Keys k="C" />.
2. Paste the link into **Links**. You can paste several, one per line.
3. **List** already says your new list. Choose **Add**.

Tonaris looks the album up and fills in its title, artist, year and cover. If it isn't sure which album you mean, it asks.

<Note kind="tip">
  **Faster:** with a list open, just paste. Press <Keys k="⌘ V" /> while no text field is selected
  and no album is open on the right, and the album goes into that list.
</Note>

## 4. Play it

Each album shows the services it has links for. Choose one, such as **Spotify**, to open the album there.

When an album has more links than fit, **+2** or similar opens the rest. Or select the album and press <Keys k="O" /> for **Open in…**, which lists them all.

## 5. Make it yours

In **Settings**, then **Preferences**:

- **Open links in**: choose **App** to open albums in your streaming apps rather than their web pages.
- **Streaming services**: turn off the services you don't use, and put the ones you use most first.

To get to Settings, choose your name at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Or press <Keys k="G" /> then <Keys k="S" />.

## Where to go next

- Put the same album in more lists, and sort lists into folders.
- Share a list with a friend.
- Install Tonaris on your phone's Home Screen to take your collection with you.
- Press <Keys k="?" /> to see every key Tonaris has.
