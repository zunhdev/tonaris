Tonaris has no app to download from a store. Your browser installs it instead, in a few seconds. The installed app is the same Tonaris, with your collection, and it's always up to date.

## What changes once it's installed

- **Its own window,** without the browser's tabs and address bar, so there's more room for your collection.
- **Its own icon** in your Dock, Start menu, taskbar or Home Screen. Open it like any other app.
- **Links stay in the app.** On a computer, with Tonaris installed from Chrome or Edge, a Tonaris link goes to the Tonaris window that's already open, rather than opening a new one.

If Tonaris asks you to sign in inside the app, sign in once. The app then remembers you.

## On a computer

### Chrome

Works on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebooks.

1. Open Tonaris in Chrome.
2. Choose the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or open Chrome's menu **⋮**, then **Cast, save, and share**, then **Install page as app…** (it may read **Install Tonaris…**).
3. Choose **Install**.

### Microsoft Edge

1. Open Tonaris in Edge.
2. Choose **App available** at the right end of the address bar. Or open Edge's menu **…**, then **Apps**, then **Install this site as an app**.
3. Choose **Install**.

If the menu doesn't offer it, your workplace may have turned installing apps off.

### Safari on a Mac

Needs macOS Sonoma or later.

1. Open Tonaris in Safari.
2. Choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
3. Choose **Add**.

### Firefox

Firefox doesn't offer to install Tonaris as an app. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari for that; in Firefox, Tonaris works as a tab.

## On a phone or tablet

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open Tonaris in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**. If you don't see it, tap **⋯** beside the address first, then **Share**.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**. Leave **Open as Web App** on.
4. Tap **Add**.

Tonaris then opens full screen from its icon. In the installed app, pull down from the top of a view to reload it.

On an iPhone or iPad, Tonaris suggests this itself: a bar at the bottom of the screen with **Install**, which shows these steps. Close it with **×**, and it won't come back on that device.

If you opened Tonaris from another app, such as Threads, Instagram or Telegram, its browser can't add Tonaris to your Home Screen. Tap **Install**, then **Copy link**, and open the link in Safari.

### Android

1. Open Tonaris in Chrome.
2. Tap Chrome's menu **⋮**, then **Add to home screen**.
3. Tap **Install**. Depending on your Chrome, the menu may read **Install app** instead.

On a phone, Tonaris is for looking through your collection, searching it and opening albums. See [Tonaris on your phone](/help/phone/on-your-phone).

## Remove the app

Removing the app never touches your collection. It stays in Tonaris, and you can sign in from any browser.

- **Chrome or Edge on a computer:** in the Tonaris window, open the menu, then choose **Uninstall Tonaris** (in Edge, **App settings** has it too).
- **Safari on a Mac:** remove Tonaris from your Dock, and drag it from the Applications folder in your home folder to the Bin.
- **iPhone or iPad:** touch and hold the Tonaris icon, then tap **Remove App**, then **Delete**.
- **Android:** touch and hold the Tonaris icon, then tap **Uninstall** or **Remove**.
