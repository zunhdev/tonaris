## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The privacy notice names the profile
The privacy notice SHALL name the avatar color and the About text among what Tonaris keeps, and say that a person's people see them, under a new `termsVersion` that asks every member again (FR-11.14, FR-11.16, TS-134).

#### Scenario: Reading the notice
- **WHEN** someone opens `/privacy`
- **THEN** it says the About text and avatar color are kept, and that the people you share with see them
