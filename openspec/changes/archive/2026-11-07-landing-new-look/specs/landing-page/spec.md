# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The landing page's pictures keep up with the app's look
The landing page's pictures SHALL show the app's current look (DS-120, TON-176): covers with their edge and shadow, an open album's ring and title, release labels, folders and lists in their colors with the tree's guide lines, the round avatar, frosted menus, filled fields, the album panel's wash and Properties, and a folder's cards with fanned covers.

#### Scenario: The workspace picture shows a grid
- **WHEN** a visitor looks at the picture under the hero on a desktop
- **THEN** it shows the list Late night as covers with labels on its EPs and live album, Blue Hour Sessions open with its ring, and the panel with its wash, list chips in their colors, and Properties

#### Scenario: On a phone
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the landing page 390 px wide
- **THEN** the pictures fit the screen, with no sideways scrolling
