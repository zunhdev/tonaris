## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Guest cards give long names a second line
On a guest page, an album card SHALL show its title and its artist on up to two lines each, and its year with its service links right after the artist (DS-22, DS-72, TON-280, TON-284).

#### Scenario: A guest's card for a long title
- **WHEN** a guest views a shared list as cards holding 'Border: Day One • 1st Mini Album' from 2020
- **THEN** the title takes two lines, and "2020" and its links follow right after the artist
