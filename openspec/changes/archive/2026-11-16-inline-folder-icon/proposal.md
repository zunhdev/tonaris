# Proposal

## Why

Since TON-283 a folder's or list's name wraps in a block beside its icon, so its second line and the count start 22 px in from the cover's edge, and the words look off the artwork. The creator picked variant 8B on 16 November 2026: the icon in the name's first line. Linear: TON-285.

## What Changes

- The icon sits in the name's first line; a second line starts at the card's left edge, as the count does (DS-41).
- Every line of words lines up with the cover, as album cards' do. Guest folder cards follow (DS-72).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `views-and-ordering`: a folder or list card's icon sits in its name's first line.
